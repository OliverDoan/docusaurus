# Ngày 17 – Relative Clauses

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Các từ quan hệ (Relative Pronouns)

| Relative Pronoun | Dùng cho | Ví dụ |
|----------------|----------|-------|
| who | người | The man who lives next door is a doctor. |
| which | vật, con vật, sự việc | The book which I bought is interesting. |
| that | người hoặc vật (bắt buộc hoặc không cần who/which) | The car that I bought is new. |
| whose | sở hữu | The girl whose name is Anna is my friend. |
| where | địa điểm | The restaurant where we ate was expensive. |
| when | thời gian | The day when we met was special. |
| why | lý do | The reason why I called is important. |

### Cấu trúc Relative Clauses

#### Defining Relative Clauses (cần thiết để xác định)
```
This is the book [which/that] I bought.
The man [who/that] lives here is my uncle.
```
- Không dùng dấu phẩy
- Dùng who/which/that

#### Non-defining Relative Clauses (thông tin thêm)
```
My father, who is 60, works at a bank.
The book, which was published in 2000, is famous.
```
- Dùng dấu phẩy
- Không dùng "that", phải dùng who/which

### Các quy tắc quan trọng

1. **Khi dùng "that" thay thế who/which**
   - Sau các từ: all, everything, nothing, something
     - All that glitters is not gold.
     - Everything that happened was recorded.
   - Sau so sánh nhất
     - This is the best movie that I've seen.

2. **Khi không dùng "that"**
   - Sau dấu phẩy (non-defining clauses)
     - My father, who is 60, is kind. (đúng)
     - My father, that is 60, is kind. (sai)

3. **Relative clauses của vật và con vật**
   - Dùng **which** (thường dùng)
   - Dùng **that** (được chấp nhận)
   - Không dùng **who**
     - The dog which/that barks is mine. (đúng)
     - The dog who barks is mine. (sai)

4. **Omission của Relative Pronouns**
   - Có thể bỏ khi làm tân ngữ
     - The book (which) I bought is interesting.
     - The man (who) I saw was tall.
   - Không thể bỏ khi làm chủ ngữ
     - The man who lives here is kind.

### Reduced Relative Clauses

#### V-ing (đang diễn ra)
```
The man who is standing there is my uncle. → The man standing there is my uncle.
```

#### V3 (bị động)
```
The letters which were sent yesterday arrived. → The letters sent yesterday arrived.
```

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### A person you admire
```
I would like to talk about my grandfather, who is the person I admire
the most in my life. He is a kind and wise man who has taught me many
important lessons.

My grandfather, who is now 85 years old, has lived through many
historical events. He was born in a difficult time when our country
was at war. Despite the challenges that he faced, he worked hard
and built a successful career.

The qualities that I admire most in my grandfather are his patience,
wisdom, and generosity. He is someone who always has time for
others. Whenever anyone in our family needs help, he is the first
person who offers assistance. The patience that he shows with children
is remarkable, and the stories that he tells us are full of wisdom.

He is also a person who has never stopped learning. Even at his age,
he reads books and keeps himself informed about what is happening
in the world. He often says that the day when a person stops
learning is the day when they start dying.

The advice that he has given me throughout my life has been
invaluable. He taught me the importance of hard work, honesty, and
treating others with respect. The values that he instilled in me have
shaped the person that I have become today.

My grandfather, whose health is not as strong as before, still
maintains a positive attitude. He is the one who keeps our family
together during difficult times. The love and support that he provides
give us all strength.

I hope that I can become the kind of person who my grandfather is.
He is not only a relative but also a mentor and a role model.
The qualities that he possesses are rare, and I feel lucky to have
him in my life.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: A Person You Admire

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. Who is a person you admire?
2. Why do you admire this person?
3. What qualities does this person have?
4. What has this person done for you?
5. How has this person influenced your life?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I would like to talk about my [relationship], who is..."

2. **Who is this person** (30 giây)
   - "[Name] is a [quality] person who..."
   - "He/She is someone who..."

3. **Why I admire them** (60 giây)
   - "The qualities that I admire most are..."
   - "I admire him/her because..."
   - Dùng relative clauses nhiều nhất có thể

4. **Influence on me** (45 giây)
   - "The advice that he/she gave me..."
   - "The values that he/she taught..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I would like to talk about my father, who is the person I admire the
most in my life. He is a hardworking and kind man who has always
supported me in everything I do.

My father, who works as an engineer, has faced many challenges in his
life but has never given up. He grew up in a poor family where education
was not easily accessible. Despite the difficulties that he faced, he
worked hard and earned a university scholarship. The determination that
he showed during those difficult times inspires me every day.

The qualities that I admire most in my father are his patience,
responsibility, and generosity. He is someone who always puts his
family first. No matter how busy he is at work, he finds time to help
us with our problems. The love and care that he shows for my mother and
me are unconditional.

My father is also a person who values education. He has always
encouraged me to study hard and pursue my dreams. The sacrifices that
he made to ensure that I could get a good education are countless.
He often says that education is the best gift that a parent can give
to their children.

The lessons that I have learned from my father have shaped who I am
today. He taught me the importance of honesty, integrity, and treating
others with respect. The advice that he gives me is always practical
and helpful. When I face difficult situations, I remember the words
that he once told me: "Never give up, no matter how hard it gets."

I hope that one day I can become the kind of parent who my father is.
I want to be someone who supports my family and passes on the values
that he taught me. My father, whose influence on my life is
immeasurable, will always be my role model.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **who**: người, **which**: vật, **that**: người/vật
- **whose**: sở hữu, **where**: địa điểm, **when**: thời gian
- **that**: dùng sau all, everything, so sánh nhất
- **Non-defining**: dùng dấu phẩy, không dùng that
- **Omission**: có thể bỏ khi làm tân ngữ
