# Ngày 23 – Prepositions

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Prepositions of Time (Giới từ thời gian)

| Preposition | Dùng khi | Ví dụ |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| at | giờ cụ thể, thời điểm | at 5 PM, at noon, at night |
| on | ngày, tuần | on Monday, on weekends |
| in | tháng, năm, mùa | in January, in 2024, in summer |
| since | kể từ | since 2020, since yesterday |
| for | trong (khoảng thời gian) | for 2 years, for a week |
| during | trong suốt (sự kiện) | during the meeting |
| by | trước, đến | by 5 PM, by tomorrow |
| until | cho đến khi | until tomorrow |
| from... to | từ... đến | from 2020 to 2024 |

**Lưu ý:**
- in the morning/afternoon/evening
- at night
- on weekdays/weekends

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Travel experiences
```
I have had several memorable travel experiences in my life. Traveling
is one of my greatest passions, and I try to visit new places
whenever I have the opportunity.

One of my most memorable trips was to Japan last year. I flew to
Tokyo with my family in spring. We spent a week exploring the city
and learning about Japanese culture. During our stay, we visited many
famous landmarks such as the Tokyo Tower and the Senso-ji Temple.
We also experienced traditional Japanese cuisine, which was
delicious and very different from what I eat at home.

Another unforgettable journey was to my hometown, Hanoi, after I had
been living abroad for several years. I traveled back by train
and arrived at the station early in the morning. As I walked through
the familiar streets, I felt a deep sense of nostalgia. I visited
my childhood home and met my old friends. We talked about our
lives and shared memories from our school days.

I have also traveled to several beaches in Vietnam. One of my
favorite destinations is Da Nang, which has beautiful beaches
like My Khe and Non Nuoc. I have been there three times, and
each visit was better than the previous one. During my last trip,
I stayed at a resort near the beach and enjoyed swimming every
day. I also tried local seafood, which is famous in that region.

Traveling to foreign countries has given me valuable experiences.
I have visited Thailand, Singapore, and Malaysia. Each country has
its own unique culture, traditions, and cuisine. Through these
travels, I have learned that despite our differences, people
around the world share similar hopes and dreams.

One challenging travel experience was when I got lost in a foreign
city. I was wandering alone in Bangkok and couldn't find my way
back to the hotel. Fortunately, a local resident saw my confusion
and helped me. This experience taught me the importance of being
prepared and the kindness of strangers.

In conclusion, traveling has enriched my life in many ways. It has
broadened my perspective, allowed me to meet interesting people,
and created memories that I will cherish forever. I look forward
to more adventures in the future and hope to explore more countries
around the world.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Travel Experiences

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. Do you like traveling? Why?
2. Where have you traveled recently?
3. What is your most memorable travel experience?
4. Do you prefer traveling alone or with others?
5. What is your dream destination?
6. What do you like most about traveling?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I am very passionate about traveling..."

2. **Recent travel** (45 giây)
   - "Recently, I traveled to..."
   - "During my trip..."

3. **Most memorable experience** (60 giây)
   - "My most memorable trip was..."
   - "I visited..." / "I stayed at..."

4. **Preferences** (30 giây)
   - "I prefer traveling..."
   - "What I like most is..."

5. **Dream destination & conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "My dream destination is..."
   - "Overall, traveling..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I am very passionate about traveling. Exploring new places and
experiencing different cultures is one of my favorite activities.
I try to travel at least once or twice a year whenever I have the
time and money.

Recently, I traveled to Da Nang, a beautiful coastal city in
central Vietnam. I went there with my family during our summer
vacation. We stayed at a resort near My Khe beach for five days.
During our trip, we spent most of our time swimming and relaxing
by the ocean. The weather was perfect, and the beach was
beautiful and clean.

My most memorable travel experience was a trip to Japan last year.
I traveled to Tokyo with my best friend, and we spent a week exploring
the city. We visited famous attractions like the Tokyo Tower,
Senso-ji Temple, and the Imperial Palace. The Japanese culture was
fascinating, and we enjoyed trying traditional food like sushi
and ramen. One highlight of the trip was visiting Mount Fuji
and watching the sunrise from there.

I prefer traveling with friends or family rather than alone. For
me, traveling is about sharing experiences with people I care about.
When I'm with others, we can create memories together and help
each other navigate unfamiliar places. However, I wouldn't mind
traveling alone once in a while to challenge myself and have some
independent time.

My dream destination is Europe, particularly France and Italy. I have
always wanted to visit Paris and see the Eiffel Tower. I also dream
of exploring Rome, Florence, and Venice to experience the rich
history, art, and cuisine of these beautiful cities.

What I like most about traveling is the opportunity to learn about
different cultures, meet new people, and see beautiful places. Each
trip teaches me something new and helps me grow as a person.
Overall, traveling has enriched my life and created countless
memories that I will always cherish.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Time**: at (giờ), on (ngày), in (tháng/năm)
- **Place**: at (vị trí), on (bề mặt), in (trong)
- **Movement**: to, into, out of, through, across
- **Position**: in, on, under, above, between, among
- **Lưu ý**: in the morning, at night, on weekends
