# Ngày 8 – Future Continuous

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + will be + V-ing
- **Phủ định**: S + will not be + V-ing
- **Nghi vấn**: Will + S + be + V-ing?

### Khi nào dùng Future Continuous?
1. **Mô tả hành động đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm tương lai**
   - At this time tomorrow, I will be flying to London.
   - At 9 AM tomorrow, she will be working.

2. **Mô tả hành động sẽ đang diễn ra khi hành động khác xảy ra**
   - When you arrive, I will be cooking dinner.
   - While you are sleeping, I will be working.

3. **Dự đoán hành động đang diễn ra bây giờ (nhưng không chắc chắn)**
   - Don't call him now. He will be working.
   - She won't be at home. She'll be shopping.

4. **Hành động diễn ra theo lịch trình**
   - We will be staying at the hotel next week.
   - I will be attending the conference next month.

### So sánh với các thì tương lai khác

| Future Simple | Future Continuous | Future Perfect |
|---------------|-------------------|----------------|
| I will finish tomorrow. | I will be working all day. | I will have finished by 5 PM. |
| Kết quả tương lai | Quá trình tương lai | Hoàn thành trước mốc |
| Tomorrow, I will call you. | At 8 AM, I will be driving. | By 5 PM, I will have arrived. |

## 📖 Từ vựng (Future Events & Activities)

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Life in 3 years
```
I often imagine what my life will be like in 3 years. In 3 years, I will be
working as a senior software developer at a large technology company. I will be
leading a team of junior developers and helping them on various projects.

At that time, I will be living in a bigger apartment, probably in the city
center. I will be enjoying the convenience of urban life - easy access to
restaurants, shopping centers, and entertainment venues.

In 3 years, I will be continuing my learning journey. I will be studying
advanced topics in computer science, especially in areas like machine learning
and cloud computing. I will be taking online courses and attending workshops
to improve my skills.

I will also be spending more time with my family. My parents will be getting
older, so I will be visiting them more often. I will be helping them with
various tasks and spending quality time together.

Financially, I will be more stable. I will be saving money regularly and
planning for the future. I might be thinking about buying a house or starting
a family.

Overall, I believe I will be living a fulfilling life. I will be doing work
that I enjoy, having good relationships with my family and friends, and
continuing to grow as a person.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Life in 3 Years

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What will you be doing this time tomorrow?
2. Where will you be living in 3 years?
3. What will you be working on next year?
4. What will you be doing when I arrive?
5. How will you be spending your weekends next month?
6. What skills will you be learning in the future?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "Let me tell you about my life in 3 years."

2. **Career** (45 giây)
   - "In 3 years, I will be..."
   - "I will be working as..."

3. **Living situation** (30 giây)
   - "I will be living..."
   - "I will be staying at..."

4. **Activities & learning** (45 giây)
   - "I will be learning..."
   - "I will be doing..."

5. **Personal life** (30 giây)
   - "I will be spending time..."
   - "I will be visiting..."

### Sample Answer:
```
Let me describe what my life might be like in 3 years. At that time, I will be
working as a senior developer at my company. I will be managing several important
projects and mentoring younger colleagues. The work will be challenging, but I
will be enjoying the responsibility.

In 3 years, I will be living in a different city. I plan to move to Ho Chi
Minh City next year because there are more job opportunities there. By then,
I will be settled in a nice apartment, probably in a convenient area near my
workplace.

Professionally, I will be continuously learning and improving. I will be
studying new technologies and keeping up with industry trends. I might be
taking specialized courses in areas like cloud computing or data science. I
will also be attending conferences and networking with other professionals.

In my personal life, I will be spending more quality time with my family and
friends. Work-life balance will be important to me, so I will be making sure
to have time for relaxation and hobbies. I will be traveling more, both for
work and leisure.

I will also be focusing on my health. I will be exercising regularly and
maintaining a healthy diet. I might be practicing yoga or meditation to
reduce stress and stay mentally healthy.

Overall, I believe I will be living a balanced and fulfilling life in 3 years.
I will be doing meaningful work, maintaining good relationships, and continuing
to grow and develop as a person.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

1. At this time tomorrow, I ______ (fly) to Paris.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- Cấu trúc: will be + V-ing
- Dùng cho: hành động đang diễn ra tại thời điểm tương lai
- Kết hợp với: at this time tomorrow, at [time], when, while
- So sánh với will: nhấn vào quá trình, không kết quả
- Hay dùng để dự đoán hành động đang diễn ra bây giờ
