# Ngày 9 – Future Perfect

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + will have + V3 (past participle)
- **Phủ định**: S + will not have + V3
- **Nghi vấn**: Will + S + have + V3?

### Khi nào dùng Future Perfect?
1. **Hành động sẽ hoàn thành trước một thời điểm tương lai**
   - By next year, I will have finished my degree.
   - By 5 PM tomorrow, I will have completed the project.

2. **Dự đoán/hy vọng điều gì đã xảy ra vào thời điểm tương lai**
   - I hope I will have found a job by next month.
   - By the time you arrive, I will have finished cooking.

3. **Khi nhấn mạnh vào kết quả, không thời gian**
   - I will have read 10 books by the end of this month. (nhấn số lượng)
   - She will have visited 5 countries by next year. (nhấn thành tích)

### Cấu trúc thường gặp

```
By + [thời điểm] + , + [chủ ngữ] + will have + V3
```

Ví dụ:
- By 2025, I will have graduated.
- By tomorrow morning, I will have finished the report.
- By the time you return, I will have cleaned the house.

### So sánh với các thì tương lai khác

| Thì | Ý nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Future Simple | Hành động tương lai | I will finish tomorrow. |
| Future Continuous | Đang diễn ra tương lai | I will be working all day. |
| Future Perfect | Hoàn thành trước mốc | I will have finished by 5 PM. |
| Future Perfect Continuous | Tiếp diễn đến mốc | I will have been working for 5 hours. |

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Goals before age 30
```
I have set several goals that I hope to achieve before I turn 30. By the time
I reach 30, I will have accomplished many things in my life.

First, in terms of education and career, I will have completed my master's
degree in Computer Science by the time I'm 28. By age 30, I will have worked
as a software developer for at least 8 years. I will have gained enough
experience to become a senior developer or even a team leader.

Professionally, I will have worked on various interesting projects. By then,
I will have developed several applications and contributed to important
software solutions. I will have also improved my skills in multiple programming
languages and technologies. I will have earned professional certifications
in areas like cloud computing and data science.

Financially, I will have saved a significant amount of money. By age 30,
I will have established a solid financial foundation for my future. I will
have started investing regularly and built an emergency fund. I might have even
purchased my own apartment.

In terms of personal growth, I will have traveled to at least 10 different
countries. I will have experienced different cultures and improved my language
skills. I will have read hundreds of books and expanded my knowledge in various
fields. I will have developed good habits like regular exercise and meditation.

I also hope to have built strong relationships. By then, I will have maintained
close connections with my family and friends. I will have made new friends
from different walks of life and built a strong professional network.

Overall, by the time I turn 30, I will have grown significantly as a person.
I will have become more knowledgeable, experienced, and confident. I will have
laid a strong foundation for the next stage of my life.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Goals Before Age 30

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What do you hope to have achieved by age 30?
2. What will you have done by next year?
3. What will you have learned by the time you graduate?
4. What goals will you have reached in 5 years?
5. Where will you have lived by that time?
6. What skills will you have mastered in the future?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I have several goals I want to achieve before age 30."

2. **Career goals** (45 giây)
   - "By age 30, I will have..."
   - "By then, I will have..."

3. **Financial goals** (30 giây)
   - "I will have saved..."
   - "I will have established..."

4. **Personal growth** (45 giây)
   - "I will have learned..."
   - "I will have traveled to..."

5. **Conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "By that time, I will have..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I have many goals that I hope to achieve before I turn 30. Let me share some
of them with you.

In terms of my career, by age 30, I will have become a senior software
engineer. I will have worked in the industry for at least 8 years and gained
extensive experience in various technologies. By then, I will have completed
several important projects and possibly led a team of developers. I will have
also earned professional certifications in areas like cloud computing and
data science.

Financially, I will have established a solid foundation. By the time I'm 30,
I will have saved enough money to purchase my own apartment. I will have started
investing regularly and built an investment portfolio. I will have also cleared
any student loans I might have and established a good credit score.

In terms of personal growth, I will have learned new skills outside of my
profession. By age 30, I will have learned to speak at least one foreign
language fluently. I will have also developed hobbies like photography or
playing a musical instrument. I will have read many books and expanded my
knowledge in various fields.

I will have traveled to several countries by then. I hope to have visited
at least 10 different countries and experienced different cultures. These
experiences will have broadened my perspective and helped me understand the
world better.

Finally, I will have maintained good relationships with my family and friends.
By then, I will have built a strong support network and made lasting connections
with people who share similar values and goals.

Overall, I believe that by age 30, I will have grown significantly as a person
and laid a strong foundation for the rest of my life.
```

### Bài tập 1: Chia động từ đúng dạng (Future Perfect)
1. By next year, I ______ (finish) my degree.
2. By 5 PM, she ______ (complete) the project.
3. By the time you arrive, I ______ (finish) cooking.
4. By next month, they ______ (move) to a new house.
5. By 2030, we ______ (solve) this problem.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- Cấu trúc: will have + V3
- Dùng cho: hành động hoàn thành trước mốc tương lai
- Thường đi với: by the time, by [date], by then, by the end of
- Nhấn vào kết quả/hoàn thành, không thời gian
- So sánh với các thì tương lai khác về ý nghĩa
