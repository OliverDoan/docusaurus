## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Education system
```
I would like to talk about the education system in my country.
Overall, our education system has many strengths, but it also has some
weaknesses that need to be addressed.

First, I believe that our primary education is quite good. Children
start school at age six and receive a solid foundation in basic subjects
such as mathematics, science, and languages. Furthermore, primary schools
are free and accessible to everyone. However, there are some problems.
For example, class sizes are often too large, which means teachers cannot
give enough individual attention to each student.

Secondary education, which includes middle and high school, is also
generally good. Students have the opportunity to study various subjects
and prepare for university entrance exams. On the other hand, there is
too much emphasis on exam results. Consequently, students often feel
stressed and focus only on memorizing facts rather than developing
critical thinking skills.

Higher education is another important aspect of our system. We have
many universities and colleges that offer a wide range of programs.
In addition, there are scholarships available for talented students from
poor families. Nevertheless, the cost of higher education can be very
high. As a result, many students graduate with significant debt,
which can affect their future decisions.

One area where our education system could improve is vocational training.
While we do have some vocational schools, they are not as popular
as academic programs. In contrast, many developed countries place great
importance on vocational education. I believe we should offer more
diverse options for students who are not interested in academic paths.

Another concern is the quality of teachers. Although we have many
dedicated and qualified teachers, their salaries are relatively low.
Therefore, it is sometimes difficult to attract and retain the best
talent in this profession. Additionally, teachers often have large
classes and heavy workloads, which can lead to burnout.

On the positive side, our education system has been improving in recent
years. Technology is being integrated into classrooms, and new teaching
methods are being explored. For instance, online learning became more
common during the pandemic. Moreover, the government has increased
funding for education in some areas.

In conclusion, while our education system has many strengths, there are
areas that need improvement. The quality of primary education is
generally good, and higher education offers many opportunities. However,
we need to reduce class sizes, improve teacher conditions, and promote
vocational training. Overall, education is the foundation of our
society, and investing in it is investing in our future.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Education System

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. Describe the education system in your country.
2. What are the strengths of your education system?
3. What are the weaknesses?
4. How could the education system be improved?
5. Compare your education system with another country's.

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I would like to talk about the education system in..."

2. **Strengths** (45 giây)
   - "First, the strengths are..."
   - "For example..."

3. **Weaknesses** (45 giây)
   - "However, there are also weaknesses."
   - "On the other hand..."

4. **Suggestions for improvement** (45 giây)
   - "I believe we could improve..."
   - "In addition..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I would like to talk about the education system in Vietnam. Overall,
our system has made significant progress, but there are still areas that
need improvement.

The strengths of our education system include the importance placed on
academic achievement. Vietnamese students are generally well-disciplined and
hardworking. Furthermore, primary education is free and accessible to
everyone. In addition, the government has invested heavily in
education infrastructure, building many new schools and universities.

However, there are also several weaknesses. One major issue is the
emphasis on exam results. Consequently, students and teachers often
focus too much on preparing for tests rather than developing
practical skills and critical thinking. Moreover, class sizes are often
too large, especially in public schools. As a result, teachers cannot
give enough individual attention to each student.

Another concern is the quality of vocational training. While academic
education is strong, vocational programs are not as well-developed.
In contrast, many developed countries have excellent vocational systems
that prepare students for specific careers. This creates a mismatch
between the skills students graduate with and the needs of the labor
market.

The education system could be improved in several ways. First, we should
reduce class sizes to allow for better teacher-student interaction.
Furthermore, the curriculum should be updated to include more practical
skills and project-based learning. In addition, we need to improve the
quality and attractiveness of vocational training to provide diverse
career paths for students.

Another area for improvement is teacher training and compensation.
Teachers play a crucial role in education, yet their salaries are often
not high enough to attract the best talent. Therefore, raising
teacher salaries and providing better professional development would help
improve the overall quality of education.

In conclusion, while Vietnam's education system has many strengths,
it needs to evolve to meet the demands of the modern world. By
addressing issues such as exam pressure, class sizes, vocational
training, and teacher conditions, we can create a more balanced and
effective system that better serves all students.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

### Bài tập 1: Điền linking words thích hợp
1. I like tea. ________, I like coffee. (also/however/because)
2. It was raining. ________, we went out. (However/Therefore/Because)
3. I was tired, ________ I went to bed. (so/but/although)
4. I like fruits, ________ apples and bananas. (such as/therefore/however)
5. She studied hard. ________, she passed the exam. (Consequently/But/Although)

### Bài tập 2: Nối các câu với linking words
1. He was late. He missed the bus.
   → ________________________________________________

2. It's expensive. It's good quality.
   → ________________________________________________

3. She studied hard. She passed the exam.
   → ________________________________________________

4. I like sports. Football and basketball.
   → ________________________________________________

5. It was raining. We played football.
   → ________________________________________________

### Bài tập 3: Viết đoạn văn với linking words
Sử dụng các linking words để nối ý trong đoạn văn sau:
```
Education is important. It helps people get jobs. It improves
critical thinking. Some countries have good systems. Others have
problems. We should invest in education.
```

### Bài tập 4: Nói về một chủ đề
Viết 5-7 câu về một chủ đề (môi trường, giao thông, sức khỏe),
dùng nhiều linking words.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Bổ sung**: also, in addition, furthermore, moreover
- **So sánh**: however, but, although, on the other hand, in contrast
- **Nguyên nhân**: because, since, so, therefore, consequently
- **Ví dụ**: for example, for instance, such as
- **Kết luận**: therefore, in conclusion, overall, all in all
