# Ngày 22 – Subject-Verb Agreement

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Quy tắc cơ bản

| Chủ ngữ | Động từ | Ví dụ |
|---------|----------|-------|
| I, You, We, They, danh từ số nhiều | V (nguyên thể) | I work, They play |
| He, She, It, tên riêng số ít | V-s/es) | He works, She plays |

### Động từ bất quy tắc

| Động từ | Chủ ngữ | Ví dụ |
|---------|----------|-------|
| be | I am | You/We/They are | He/She/It is |
| have | I/You/We/They have | He/She/It has |
| do | I/You/We/They do | He/She/It does |
| go | I/You/We/They go | He/She/It goes |

### Các trường hợp đặc biệt

#### Chủ ngữ số ít kết thúc bằng -s
- The bus **goes** to the station.
- The news **is** important. (news luôn số ít)
- Mathematics **is** difficult. (tên môn học số ít)

#### Các từ luôn số nhiều
- People **are** friendly.
- Police **are** investigating.
- Cattle **are** grazing.
- Clothes **are** expensive.

#### Mỗi... mỗi (Each, Every) luôn số ít
- Each student **has** a book.
- Every person **needs** help.
- Everyone **is** welcome.
- Everything **is** ready.

#### Cả hai... (Both, Neither, Either + and/or)
- Both A and B **are**...
  - Both cats and dogs **are** popular pets.
- Neither A nor B: theo chủ ngữ gần nhất
  - Neither my sister nor I **am** going.
  - Neither my brothers nor I **are** going.
- Either A or B: theo chủ ngữ gần nhất
  - Either he or I **am** right.

#### Một trong những (One of the + danh từ số nhiều)
- One of the students **is** absent.
- One of my friends **has** called me.

#### Một vài (Some of + danh từ số nhiều/số ít tùy)
- Some of the water **is** polluted.
- Some of the students **are** here.

#### Phần lớn (A lot of, lots of)
- A lot of people **are** coming.
- A lot of water **is** wasted.

#### Số lượng (The number of / A number of)
- The number of students **is** increasing. (số ít)
- A number of students **are** absent. (số nhiều = nhiều)

#### Mỗi danh từ (The + danh từ số ít + of + danh từ số nhiều)
- The list of names **is** long.
- The collection of stamps **is** valuable.

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Work and career
```
I would like to talk about my career and work life. I work as a
software developer for a technology company. My company **is** located
in Hanoi, and it **has** been operating for over ten years.

My job involves creating and maintaining software applications. Every
day, I write code and test programs. I also collaborate with other
developers who **are** working on the same projects. My team **consists**
of five people, and we **work** closely together.

The company **provides** several benefits to its employees. For
example, we **have** health insurance and paid vacations. The
salary **is** competitive, and there **are** opportunities for
promotion. Every employee **receives** regular training to improve
their skills.

However, the work **can be** challenging sometimes. We often
**have** tight deadlines, and we **need** to work extra hours to meet
them. When a major project **approaches**, everyone in the team
**becomes** very busy. Despite the pressure, I enjoy my work
because it **is** both rewarding and interesting.

My manager **is** very supportive. She **understands** when we **have**
personal problems and **tries** to help us balance our work and life.
My colleagues **are** friendly and professional. We **have** a good
working relationship, and we **support** each other when needed.

In terms of career development, the company **offers** various
opportunities. I **have** taken several courses to improve my
programming skills. The company **pays** for these courses, which
**is** very generous. I **believe** that investing in employee
development **benefits** both the individual and the organization.

Looking at the future, I **hope** to advance in my career. My goal
**is** to become a senior developer within the next few years.
There **are** many opportunities for growth in my current company,
and I **plan** to take advantage of them. The industry **is**
evolving rapidly, and I **want** to keep up with the latest
technologies.

Overall, my work **is** an important part of my life. It **provides**
me with financial stability, intellectual challenges, and opportunities
for growth. I **am** grateful for the job I **have**, and I **plan**
to continue working hard and developing my career.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Work and Career

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What do you do for a living?
2. Do you enjoy your job? Why?
3. What are the main responsibilities in your job?
4. What would you like to change about your job?
5. What are your career goals?
6. Do you think you will stay in this job for a long time?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **What you do** (30 giây)
   - "I work as..."
   - "My job involves..."

2. **What you like** (45 giây)
   - "I enjoy my job because..."
   - "The thing I like most is..."

3. **Challenges** (30 giây)
   - "However, there are challenges..."
   - "Sometimes it can be..."

4. **Colleagues & environment** (30 giây)
   - "My colleagues are..."
   - "The working environment is..."

5. **Future goals** (30 giây)
   - "In the future, I hope to..."
   - "My career goal is to..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I work as a software developer for a technology company. My job
involves creating, testing, and maintaining computer programs. I write
a lot of code and solve technical problems every day.

I enjoy my job for several reasons. First, it is intellectually
stimulating. I face new challenges every day, which keeps me interested
and motivated. Second, the salary is good, and the company provides
several benefits like health insurance and paid vacations. Furthermore,
I have great colleagues who are supportive and friendly. We work as a
team and help each other when needed.

However, there are some challenges. Sometimes the deadlines are
very tight, which means we have to work late nights. The pressure can
be stressful, especially when we encounter difficult bugs or
technical problems. Also, the technology industry changes rapidly, so
I need to keep learning new things constantly.

My colleagues are professional and easy to work with. My manager is
very understanding and supportive. The company culture is positive,
and they encourage us to develop our skills. We have regular training
sessions, which helps us improve professionally.

In terms of future career goals, I hope to become a senior developer
within the next three years. I also want to learn about artificial
intelligence, which is a growing field in technology. Perhaps in the
longer term, I might start my own business or become a consultant.

Overall, I am satisfied with my current job. It provides me with
good income, interesting work, and opportunities for growth. I believe
that building a career in technology is a good choice for me, and I
plan to continue in this field.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **I/You/We/They + V**, **He/She/It + V-s**
- **Mỗi (Each/Every)**: số ít
- **Both... and**: số nhiều
- **Neither... nor / Either... or**: theo chủ ngữ gần nhất
- **One of the + số nhiều**: số ít
- **A lot of + số nhiều/số ít**: tùy theo danh từ
