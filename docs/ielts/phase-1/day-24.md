### Phrasal Verbs với tân ngữ

#### Các phrasal verbs tách rời
```
I woke up early.
She looked after the children.
They ran out of money.
```

#### Các phrasal verbs không tách rời
```
I looked for my keys everywhere.
She took off her coat.
They ran into their old friend.
```

| **go out** | tắt | hết | The light went out. | We went out of food. |

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Daily life situations
```
Let me describe my typical day using various phrasal verbs. Every
morning, I **wake up** around 6:30 AM. After lying in bed for a
few minutes, I **get up** and **get dressed**. I usually **put on**
my work clothes and **head for** the bathroom where I **wash my face**
and **brush my teeth**.

After getting ready, I **sit down** for breakfast. While eating,
I often **look through** my emails and **check** any important messages.
Then I **head out** for work. I usually **take the bus** or **drive**
to my office depending on the weather.

At work, I **settle down**** at my desk and **start working on**
my tasks. Throughout the day, I **deal with** various problems
that **come up**. Sometimes I need to **figure out** difficult issues
or **look for** information online. I often **work with** my
colleagues and **help them out** when they **run into** trouble.

During lunch break, I sometimes **meet up** with coworkers or
**grab** some food from a nearby restaurant. We often **chat about**
our work and **catch up**** on each other's lives. After lunch, I
**get back to** work and **continue** until 5 PM.

After work, I **head back** home. Sometimes I **stop by** the
store to **pick up** some groceries. When I arrive home, I **put
away** my things and **take off** my work clothes. I usually
**sit down** and **relax for a while** before starting on my
evening routine.

In the evening, I might **clean up** the house, **do some
laundry**, or **work on** a personal project. I also try to **spend
time** with my family. We often **eat dinner together** and **talk
about our day**. Sometimes we **watch TV** or **play games**.

Before going to bed, I usually **lay out** my clothes for the next
day and **pack my bag** if I need to go anywhere. I **set my
alarm**** for the next morning and **lie down** to sleep. **Looking
back on** my day, I try to **make the most of** every moment
and **handle whatever** challenges **come my way**.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Daily Life Situations

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What is your morning routine?
2. How do you handle stress at work?
3. What do you do when you encounter a problem?
4. How do you organize your time?
5. What do you do to relax after work?
6. How do you prepare for the next day?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Morning routine** (45 giây)
   - "I wake up at..."
   - "After getting up, I..."
   - Dùng nhiều phrasal verbs

2. **Work day** (45 giây)
   - "At work, I..."
   - "When problems come up, I..."
   - "I deal with..."

3. **After work** (30 giây)
   - "After finishing work, I..."
   - "I usually..."

4. **Evening & preparation** (30 giây)
   - "In the evening, I..."
   - "Before going to bed, I..."

### Sample Answer:
```
My day usually starts early. I wake up at 6:30 AM and take a few
minutes to fully wake up before getting out of bed. After getting up,
I quickly get dressed and head to the bathroom to wash my face and
brush my teeth. Then I put on my clothes and prepare for the day.

Before leaving for work, I usually sit down for breakfast and look
through my emails. I check for any urgent messages that need my
attention. After eating, I grab my bag and head out to work. I
often take the bus, especially when the weather is nice.

At work, I settle down at my desk and start working on my tasks.
Throughout the day, I deal with various situations that come up.
Sometimes I run into difficult problems and need to figure out solutions.
I often work with my colleagues and help them out when they face
challenges. We also catch up during lunch break and talk about
our work.

After finishing at 5 PM, I head back home. Sometimes I stop by
the store to pick up some groceries for dinner. When I arrive, I
put away my things and take off my work clothes. I usually sit
down and relax for a while before starting on my evening
activities.

In the evening, I might clean up around the house or do some
laundry. I also enjoy spending time with my family. We often eat
dinner together and talk about our day. Sometimes we watch TV or
play games to unwind.

Before going to bed, I usually prepare for the next day. I lay
out my clothes and pack my bag if needed. I set my alarm and try
to get to sleep at a reasonable time. Looking back on my day, I
try to make the most of every moment and handle whatever comes
my way.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Phrasal verbs**: động từ + giới từ (có nghĩa đặc biệt)
- **Tách rời**: nhiều phrasal verbs tách được ra khỏi tân ngữ
- **Không tách rời**: một số không tách (look for, take off)
- **Quan trọng**: thuộc các phrasal verbs thông dụng
- **Nhiều đa dạng**: một số phrasal verbs có nhiều nghĩa
