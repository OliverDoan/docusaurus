# Ngày 2 – Present Continuous

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + am/is/are + V-ing
- **Phủ định**: S + am/is/are + not + V-ing
- **Nghi vấn**: Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing?

### Quy tắc thêm -ing
1. Thông thường: V + ing
   - work → working, play → playing

2. Động từ kết thúc bằng **-e**: bỏ e, thêm -ing
   - make → making, write → writing, take → taking
   - Ngoại lệ: see → seeing

3. Động từ kết thúc bằng phụ âm duy nhất + **-ie**: đổi ie thành y + ing
   - lie → lying, die → dying

4. Động từ có 1 âm tiết, kết thúc phụ âm + **1 phụ âm đơn**: gấp đôi phụ âm + ing
   - run → running, swim → swimming, sit → sitting
   - Ngoại lệ: go → going

5. Động từ 2 âm tiết, nhấn âm thứ 2, kết thúc phụ âm + 1 phụ âm đơn
   - begin → beginning, forget → forgetting, prefer → preferring

### Khi nào dùng Present Continuous?
1. **Hành động đang diễn ra lúc nói**
   - I am studying right now.
   - She is talking on the phone.

2. **Hành động diễn ra quanh thời điểm hiện tại (tạm thời)**
   - I am working on a new project this week.
   - He is staying with his parents this month.

3. **Kế hoạch gần tương lai (đã sắp xếp)**
   - I am meeting my friends tomorrow.
   - We are flying to Paris next week.

4. **Diễn tả sự thay đổi, phát triển**
   - The internet is growing rapidly.
   - The weather is getting worse.

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Action happening right now
```
Right now, I am sitting in my room. My brother is playing video games.
My mother is cooking dinner in the kitchen. The birds are singing outside.
```

### Temporary activities
```
I am working on a new project this month. My friend is studying for her exams.
We are preparing for the upcoming competition. The company is expanding rapidly.
```

### Future arrangements
```
I am meeting my client tomorrow at 2 PM. We are flying to Tokyo next Friday.
My sister is getting married next month. They are moving to a new house soon.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: What are you doing these days?

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What are you doing right now?
2. What are you working on these days?
3. Are you learning anything new at the moment?
4. What are you planning to do this weekend?
5. What is your family doing now?
6. What are your friends up to these days?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Current action** (30 giây)
   - "At the moment, I am..."

2. **Recent activities this week** (45 giây)
   - "This week, I am..."

3. **Temporary situations** (45 giây)
   - "Currently, I am..."

4. **Near future plans** (45 giây)
   - "I am planning to..."

5. **Conclusion** (15 giây)
   - "That's what I've been busy with lately."

### Sample Answer:
```
Right now, I am preparing for my IELTS exam. I am studying English every day
and practicing speaking with my friends. This week, I am also working on a new
project at my office. My colleagues and I are developing a mobile application.

Currently, I am learning about web development in my free time. I am taking an
online course and working on some small projects. I am also reading a lot of
books about programming.

Next weekend, I am meeting my study group to practice speaking together.
We are planning to do some mock tests. I am really looking forward to it.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

### Bài tập 1: Chia động từ đúng dạng
1. I ______ (study) for the exam right now.
2. She ______ (not/work) at the moment.
3. They ______ (move) to a new house next week.
4. What ______ you ______ (do) these days?
5. He ______ (wait) for the bus.

### Bài tập 2: Viết câu phủ định
1. I am working on a project. → ____________________
2. She is studying English. → ____________________
3. They are playing football. → ____________________

### Bài tập 3: Viết câu nghi vấn
1. You are reading a book. → ____________________
2. He is writing an email. → ____________________
3. They are cooking dinner. → ____________________

### Bài tập 4: So sánh Present Simple và Present Continuous
Điền vào chỗ trống: do / does / am / is / are / work / works / working

1. She usually ______ (work) from home, but today she ______ (go) to the office.
2. I ______ (not/understand) what you ______ (say) right now.
3. They ______ (play) football every Sunday, but this week they ______ (not/play).
4. He ______ (teach) at university. Currently he ______ (research) in his lab.
5. My dad ______ (drink) coffee every morning. Now he ______ (drink) tea.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- Cấu trúc: am/is/are + V-ing
- Quy tắc thêm -ing: -e → -ing, -ie → -ying, gấp đôi phụ âm
- Dùng cho: hành động đang diễn ra, tạm thời, kế hoạch gần
- Từ chỉ thời điểm: now, at the moment, right now, these days
- Khác với Present Simple: Present Continuous = diễn ra/ tạm thời
