# Ngày 3 – Past Simple

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + V2 (past tense)
- **Phủ định**: S + did + not + V (nguyên thể)
- **Nghi vấn**: Did + S + V (nguyên thể)?

### Quy tắc chia động từ quá khứ thường
1. Thông thường: thêm **-ed**
   - work → worked, play → played, watch → watched

2. Động từ kết thúc bằng **-e**: chỉ thêm **-d**
   - like → liked, love → loved, dance → danced

3. Động từ kết thúc phụ âm + **-y**: đổi y thành **-ied**
   - study → studied, cry → cried, try → tried
   - Ngoại lệ: play → played, stay → stayed

4. Động từ 1 âm tiết, kết thúc phụ âm + 1 phụ âm đơn: gấp đôi phụ âm + ed
   - stop → stopped, plan → planned, shop → shopped

5. Động từ 2 âm tiết, nhấn âm thứ 2, kết thúc phụ âm + 1 phụ âm đơn
   - prefer → preferred, admit → admitted, occur → occurred

### Khi nào dùng Past Simple?
1. **Hành động đã hoàn tất trong quá khứ** (có thời gian cụ thể)
   - I visited Paris last year.
   - She graduated from university in 2020.

2. **Dãy sự kiện theo trình tự thời gian**
   - I woke up, brushed my teeth, and went to work.
   - He opened the door, walked inside, and sat down.

3. **Kể chuyện về người đã qua đời hoặc sự việc không còn tồn tại**
   - My grandfather lived in this house.
   - The castle stood on that hill.

4. **Trả lời câu hỏi When?**
   - When did you buy this car? → I bought it last month.

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### A memorable day
```
Last summer, I had a memorable day. It was my birthday. My friends organized
a surprise party for me. In the morning, they called me and asked me to meet
them at a restaurant. When I arrived, nobody was there. I felt confused and
a bit disappointed.

Suddenly, they jumped out from behind the tables and shouted "Happy Birthday!"
I was so surprised and happy. We had lunch together and talked for hours.
In the evening, we went to the beach and watched the sunset. After that, we
had dinner at a nice restaurant. It was one of the best days of my life.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: A Memorable Day

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What is your most memorable day?
2. When did it happen?
3. Who was with you?
4. What did you do on that day?
5. Why was it so memorable?
6. How did you feel?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I would like to talk about a memorable day in my life."

2. **When & Where** (30 giây)
   - "This happened..."

3. **What happened** (1 phút)
   - "On that day, I..."
   - Kể chi tiết các sự kiện theo trình tự

4. **Feelings & reactions** (30 giây)
   - "I felt..." / "It was..."

5. **Why memorable** (30 giây)
   - "This day was memorable because..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I would like to talk about my graduation day, which was one of the most memorable
days in my life. It happened two years ago, in June 2022, at my university in Hanoi.

On that day, I arrived at the university campus early in the morning. The atmosphere
was exciting and emotional. Many students wore graduation gowns and took photos
with their friends and families. I met my classmates, and we took many pictures
together to remember this special moment.

The graduation ceremony started at 9 AM. All students walked onto the stage one by one.
When my name was called, I walked up, and the principal gave me my diploma. I felt
proud and happy because I had completed four years of hard work. My parents were
in the audience, and they took photos of me.

After the ceremony, my family and I had lunch at a nice restaurant. We celebrated
my achievement and talked about my future plans. In the evening, I went to a party
with my friends. We danced and shared memories about our time at university.

This day was memorable for several reasons. First, it marked an important milestone
in my education. Second, my whole family was there to support me. Finally, it was
a day full of emotions and happiness that I will never forget.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

### Bài tập 1: Chia động từ đúng dạng
1. I ______ (visit) my grandparents last week.
2. She ______ (not/go) to the party yesterday.
3. They ______ (buy) a new car last month.
4. Where ______ you ______ (go) on vacation last year?
5. He ______ (write) a letter to his friend.

### Bài tập 2: Chọn động từ bất quy tắc đúng
1. I (see/saw/seen) him yesterday.
2. She (go/went/gone) to Paris last summer.
3. They (make/made/maked) a big mistake.
4. He (eat/ate/eaten) dinner at 7 PM.
5. We (take/took/taken) many photos.

### Bài tập 3: Sửa lỗi trong câu
1. I didn't went to school yesterday.
2. She didn't knew the answer.
3. Where you went last night?
4. They didn't wrote the email.
5. Did you saw the movie?

### Bài tập 4: Viết câu quá khứ
Viết 5 câu quá khứ mô tả những gì bạn làm vào ngày hôm qua.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- Cấu trúc: S + V2 (khẳng định), did + not + V (phủ định)
- Quy tắc -ed: -e → -d, -y → -ied, gấp đôi phụ âm
- Phải thuộc các động từ bất quy tắc
- Dùng cho: hành động đã hoàn tất, có thời gian cụ thể
- Từ chỉ thời gian: yesterday, last..., ...ago, in (năm)
