# Ngày 25 – Adverbial Clauses

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Các từ nối cho Adverbial Clauses

| Từ nối | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|--------|-------|-------|
| when | khi | When I arrived, he left. |
| while | trong khi | While I was eating, he called. |
| before | trước khi | Before I left, I said goodbye. |
| after | sau khi | After I finished, I went home. |
| until | cho đến khi | I waited until he came. |
| as soon as | ngay khi | As soon as I saw him, I waved. |
| once | một khi | Once you start, don't stop. |
| whenever | bất cứ khi nào | Whenever I visit, I buy something. |
| since | kể từ | Since I moved here, I've been happy. |
| as | khi | As I was walking, I saw him. |
| where | nơi | This is where I live. |
| wherever | bất cứ nơi nào | Wherever you go, I'll follow. |
| because | vì | I stayed because it was raining. |
| since | vì | Since you're here, let's start. |
| as | vì | As I was tired, I went to bed. |
| although | mặc dù | Although it rained, we went out. |
| though | tuy | Though tired, I continued. |
| even though | dù cho | Even though it's late, I'll go. |
| if | nếu | If it rains, I'll stay home. |
| unless | trừ khi | I won't go unless you come. |

### Position of Adverbial Clauses

#### Mệnh đề chính (thường đứng sau)
```
[Adverbial clause], [main clause]
When I arrived, the meeting had already started.
```

#### Mệnh đề chính có thể đứng trước (thêm dấu phẩy)
```
[Main clause], [adverbial clause]
I'll stay home, if it rains.
```

**Lưu ý:** Không dùng dấu phẩy khi mệnh đề chính đứng trước
```
When I arrived, the meeting had started. (đúng)
When I arrived, the meeting had started. (sai - thừa dấu phẩy)
```

### Các cấu trúc đặc biệt

#### When vs. While
- **When**: điểm thời gian
  - When I arrived, he left. (khi tôi đến)
- **While**: quá trình diễn ra
  - While I was waiting, he arrived. (trong khi tôi chờ)

#### Before vs. After
- **Before**: trước khi
  - Before I left, I locked the door.
- **After**: sau khi
  - After I locked the door, I left.

#### As soon as vs. Once
- **As soon as**: ngay lập tức
  - As soon as I heard the news, I called you.
- **Once**: một khi (nhấn vào điều kiện)
  - Once you start, don't look back.

#### Whenever vs. Wherever
- **Whenever**: bất cứ khi nào (thời gian)
  - Whenever I visit Paris, I go to the Eiffel Tower.
- **Wherever**: bất cứ nơi nào (không gian)
  - Wherever you go, I'll be there.

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Festivals
```
I would like to talk about Tet holiday, which is the most
important festival in Vietnam. Tet celebrates the Lunar New Year
and marks the beginning of spring according to the lunar calendar.

Before Tet, people are very busy preparing. While some clean their
houses thoroughly, others decorate with flowers and peach
blossoms. Families often go shopping for new clothes and gifts
for children. When everything is ready, people return to their
hometowns to celebrate with their families.

On New Year's Eve, family members gather together. As soon as
the clock strikes midnight, everyone exchanges wishes for happiness,
health, and prosperity in the coming year. The atmosphere is
filled with excitement and joy as children receive lucky money in
red envelopes from their elders.

During Tet, people visit relatives and friends. Whenever I
go to someone's house, I bring gifts and offer best wishes.
It's a time when people put aside conflicts and focus on
reconciliation and strengthening relationships. The tradition of
visiting elders and showing respect is deeply valued in our culture.

One of my favorite Tet traditions is giving lucky money. Children
receive this money in red envelopes, which symbolizes good luck
and prosperity for the new year. As a child, I always looked
forward to Tet because I could save this money and buy things
I wanted.

Another important tradition is eating special foods. Since each
region has its own specialties, Tet is a great opportunity to
enjoy traditional dishes like banh chung, sticky rice cakes,
and pickled vegetables. Families gather to prepare these
foods together, which strengthens family bonds.

Throughout Tet holiday, which lasts for about a week, people
relax and enjoy time with family. Traditional games are played,
and special performances entertain everyone. While streets are
decorated beautifully, fireworks light up the sky at night,
creating a magical atmosphere.

In conclusion, Tet is more than just a holiday. It's a
time when cultural traditions are preserved, family relationships
are strengthened, and hopes for the future are renewed. The
values and customs associated with Tet reflect the importance
that Vietnamese people place on family, community, and
tradition.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Festivals

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What is the most important festival in your country?
2. How do people celebrate this festival?
3. What traditions are associated with this festival?
4. Do you prefer traditional or modern celebrations?
5. What is your favorite memory of this festival?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I would like to talk about [festival]..."

2. **Preparation** (40 giây)
   - "Before [festival], people..."
   - "While some..., others..."

3. **Celebration activities** (50 giây)
   - "On the day of..."
   - "During the festival..."

4. **Traditions** (40 giây)
   - "One important tradition is..."
   - "Whenever I..."

5. **Personal experience & conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "For me, [festival] is special because..."
   - "Overall, this festival represents..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I would like to talk about Tet holiday, the most important
festival in Vietnam. Tet celebrates the Lunar New Year and
typically occurs in late January or early February.

Before Tet arrives, preparations begin weeks in advance. While some
people thoroughly clean their houses to sweep away bad luck from
the previous year, others focus on decorating their homes with
flowers and traditional ornaments. Families also shop for new
clothes and gifts, especially for children. When Tet approaches,
the atmosphere becomes more exciting as people complete their
preparations.

On New Year's Eve, families gather for a special dinner
called 'tan nien', which is the final meal of the year. As soon
as the clock strikes midnight, fireworks light up the sky across
the country. Family members exchange wishes for happiness, health,
and prosperity. Children receive lucky money in red envelopes,
which is a cherished tradition.

During Tet, which lasts for about a week, people visit
relatives and friends. Whenever I visit someone's house, I
bring gifts and offer traditional New Year greetings. It's a time
for strengthening relationships and showing respect to elders. People
also visit temples and pagodas to pray for a good year ahead.

One tradition I particularly enjoy is the custom of eating
special Tet foods. Since different regions have their own
specialties, I always look forward to enjoying traditional dishes
like banh chung, sticky rice cakes, and various pickled
vegetables. Preparing and sharing these foods together is an
important family activity.

For me, Tet is special because it's the only time when my
entire family gathers together. Since my relatives live in
different cities, Tet gives us an opportunity to reunite. We share
stories, meals, and create new memories together.

In conclusion, Tet represents the importance of family, tradition,
and hope for the future in Vietnamese culture. The customs and
values associated with this festival reflect our respect for elders,
love for family, and optimism for the new year.
```

- **Until**: cho đến khi
