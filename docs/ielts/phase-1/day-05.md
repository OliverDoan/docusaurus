# Ngày 5 – Present Perfect

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + have/has + V3 (past participle)
- **Phủ định**: S + have/has + not + V3
- **Nghi vấn**: Have/Has + S + V3?

### Quy tắc chia
- **have**: I, You, We, They, danh từ số nhiều
- **has**: He, She, It, tên riêng số ít

### Quy tắc thêm -ed (V3 tương tự V2)
1. Thông thường: V + ed
   - work → worked, play → played

2. Động từ kết thúc bằng **-e**: thêm **-d**
   - like → liked, love → loved

3. Động từ kết thúc phụ âm + **-y**: đổi y thành **-ied**
   - study → studied, try → tried

4. (Quy tắc gấp đôi phụ âm giống V2)

### Khi nào dùng Present Perfect?
1. **Kinh nghiệm trong quá khứ (không cần thời gian cụ thể)**
   - I have visited Paris three times.
   - She has never eaten sushi.

2. **Hành động xảy ra trong quá khứ nhưng kết quả vẫn còn đến hiện tại**
   - I have lost my keys. (Vẫn chưa tìm thấy)
   - She has graduated. (Bây giờ là cử nhân)

3. **Hành động bắt đầu trong quá khứ và kéo dài đến hiện tại**
   - I have lived here for 10 years. (Vẫn đang sống ở đây)
   - She has worked at this company since 2020. (Vẫn đang làm)

4. **Hành động vừa xảy ra gần đây (với just, already, yet)**
   - I have just finished my homework.
   - She has already left.
   - Have you done it yet?

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Life experiences
```
I have had many interesting experiences in my life. I have traveled to several
countries including Thailand, Singapore, and Japan. Last year, I visited Paris
for the first time, and it was an amazing experience.

I have tried many different hobbies. I have learned to play the guitar,
though I'm not very good at it yet. I have also tried photography and cooking.
Currently, I am focusing on improving my English skills.

In terms of career, I have worked for three different companies. I have gained
a lot of experience in project management. I have successfully completed several
important projects, and I have been promoted twice.

I have faced many challenges, but I have learned from all of them. I believe that
every experience, whether good or bad, has helped me grow as a person.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Life Experiences

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. Have you ever traveled abroad? Where did you go?
2. What is the most interesting place you have ever visited?
3. Have you ever tried an extreme sport?
4. What skills have you learned in your life?
5. What achievements are you most proud of?
6. Have you ever faced a difficult challenge? How did you overcome it?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I have had several interesting experiences in my life."

2. **Travel experiences** (40 giây)
   - "I have visited..." / "I have traveled to..."

3. **Skills & hobbies** (40 giây)
   - "I have learned..." / "I have tried..."

4. **Achievements** (40 giây)
   - "I have achieved..." / "I have accomplished..."

5. **Challenges** (30 giây)
   - "I have faced..." / "I have overcome..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I have had many memorable experiences in my life so far. One of the most
interesting experiences I have had was traveling to Japan last year. I have
visited Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka. I have tried traditional Japanese food like
sushi and ramen, and I have even learned some basic Japanese phrases.

I have also developed several skills throughout my life. I have learned to play
the piano since I was a child. I have practiced for many years, and now I can
play quite well. I have also learned swimming, which is a skill that I use
regularly. Recently, I have started learning programming, and I have already
completed a few online courses.

In terms of achievements, I have graduated from university with good grades.
I have worked on several group projects and have received positive feedback
from my professors. I have also participated in volunteer activities and have
helped organize charity events.

I have faced some challenges too, but I have learned valuable lessons from them.
I have learned that hard work and persistence are important for success.
I have also discovered that asking for help is not a sign of weakness, but a
way to grow and improve.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

### Bài tập 1: Chia động từ đúng dạng (Present Perfect)
1. I ______ (visit) Paris three times.
2. She ______ (not/eat) sushi before.
3. They ______ (live) here for 10 years.
4. ______ you ______ (see) this movie?
5. He ______ (work) at this company since 2020.

1. I ______ finished my homework.
2. She ______ lost her keys.
3. They ______ arrived safely.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- Cấu trúc: have/has + V3
- have: I, you, we, they
- has: he, she, it, tên số ít
- Dùng cho: kinh nghiệm, kết quả đến hiện tại, kéo dài đến hiện tại
- Từ chỉ thời gian: for (khoảng thời gian), since (mốc thời gian)
- ever (đã bao giờ), never (chưa bao giờ), already (đã rồi), yet (chưa), just (vừa)
