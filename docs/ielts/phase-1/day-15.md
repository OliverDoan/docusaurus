# Ngày 15 – Conditionals

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Type 0 – Conditional loại 0 (Sự thật hiển nhiên)

#### Cấu trúc
```
If + Present Simple, Present Simple
```

#### Dùng khi
- Sự thật luôn đúng, luật tự nhiên, quy tắc
- If you heat water, it boils.
- If you don't eat, you get hungry.
- If you press the button, it starts.

### Type 1 – Conditional loại 1 (Khả năng thực)

#### Cấu trúc
```
If + Present Simple, will + V
```

#### Dùng khi
- Khả năng thực tế trong tương lai
- If it rains, I will stay home.
- If she studies hard, she will pass the exam.
- If you have time, we will meet.

#### Các biến thể
```
If + Present Simple, can/may/might/should/going to + V
```
- If you are ready, we can start.
- If it's sunny, we might go to the park.

### Type 2 – Conditional loại 2 (Giả tưởng hiện tại)

#### Cấu trúc
```
If + Past Simple, would + V
```

#### Dùng khi
- Giả tưởng về hiện tại hoặc tương lai gần (không có thật)
- If I had more money, I would buy a car.
- If I were you, I would accept the offer.
- If she lived here, we would meet often.

#### Lưu ý
- Với "to be", dùng "were" với mọi ngôi
- If I were rich, I would travel the world. (không dùng was)

### Type 3 – Conditional loại 3 (Giả tưởng quá khứ)

#### Cấu trúc
```
If + Past Perfect, would have + V3
```

#### Dùng khi
- Giả tưởng về quá khứ (lời tiếc nuối, những gì đã khác)
- If I had studied harder, I would have passed.
- If she had told me, I would have helped.
- If we had left earlier, we wouldn't have been late.

### Mixed Conditionals (Câu điều kiện hỗn hợp)

#### Present → Past
```
If + Past Simple, would have + V3
```
- If I spoke French, I would have understood him.

#### Past → Present
```
If + Past Perfect, would + V
```
- If I had taken that job, I would be rich now.

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Dreams and future
```
I have many dreams for my future. If I could change one thing about my
life right now, I would have more time to pursue my passions. I love
painting and playing the guitar, but my work keeps me very busy.

If I had more money, I would travel around the world. I would visit
different countries, experience new cultures, and meet interesting people.
I would particularly like to visit France, Italy, and Japan. Traveling
has always been my biggest dream.

In terms of my career, if I get a promotion next year, I will invest in
professional development. I will take advanced courses and learn new skills.
This will help me become an expert in my field. However, if I don't get
the promotion, I will look for opportunities at other companies.

My biggest dream is to start my own business. If I had enough savings,
I would quit my job and start a tech startup. I would develop
applications that solve real problems for people. If I had started this
business five years ago, I would be running a successful company by now.

I also dream about living in a different city. If I lived near the
beach, I would wake up to the sound of waves every morning. I would
spend my free time swimming and surfing. Living by the sea has always
appealed to me.

When I think about my future, I feel optimistic. If I continue working
hard and making smart decisions, I believe all my dreams will come true.
Life is full of possibilities, and I am excited to see where my journey
takes me.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Dreams and Future

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. If you could change one thing about your life, what would it be?
2. What would you do if you had a lot of money?
3. What would you do if you could live anywhere in the world?
4. If you could have any job, what would you choose?
5. What would you have done differently in the past?
6. If you could meet anyone, who would it be?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "If I could change one thing about my life..."

2. **Dream 1** (45 giây)
   - "If I had/If I could..."
   - "I would..."

3. **Dream 2** (45 giây)
   - "Another dream of mine is..."
   - "If I had..."

4. **Career/Work dreams** (45 giây)
   - "In terms of my career..."
   - "If I get..."

5. **Conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "Overall, I believe that..."

### Sample Answer:
```
If I could change one thing about my life right now, I would have more
free time to pursue my hobbies. I enjoy painting and playing the guitar,
but work keeps me too busy. If I had more time, I would practice these
activities every day.

If I had a lot of money, I would travel around the world. I would visit
countries I've always dreamed about, like Italy, Japan, and Australia.
I would also help my family and friends. I would support my parents'
retirement and help my younger siblings with their education.

If I could live anywhere in the world, I would choose to live near the
ocean. I've always loved the sea. If I lived by the beach, I would
wake up to the sound of waves every morning. I would spend my weekends
swimming, surfing, or just relaxing by the water.

If I could have any job, I would be a professional photographer. I love
capturing beautiful moments and sharing them with others. If I were a
photographer, I would travel to different places and document cultures
and landscapes through my lens.

Thinking about what I would have done differently in the past, if I had
started learning English earlier, I would be more fluent by now. I started
learning in high school, but I wish I had begun in primary school.
This would have opened up more opportunities for me.

If I could meet anyone, alive or dead, I would want to meet my great-
grandfather. He passed away before I was born. My family always talks
about how wise and kind he was. If I could meet him, I would learn
so much about our family history and his experiences.

Overall, I believe that dreams give us direction in life. Even if not all
my dreams come true, working towards them makes life meaningful and exciting.
```

3. If she had studied, she ______ (pass) the exam.
4. If I ______ (be) you, I would accept the offer.

3. If she (had/know) ______ the answer, she (would/tell) ______ you.
4. If we (had/leave) ______ earlier, we (would/arrive) ______ on time.
5. If it (is) ______ sunny tomorrow, we (might/go) ______ to the park.

### Bài tập 4: Viết về ước mơ

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Type 0**: If + Present Simple, Present Simple (sự thật)
- **Type 1**: If + Present Simple, will + V (khả năng thực)
- **Type 2**: If + Past Simple, would + V (giả tưởng hiện tại)
- **Type 3**: If + Past Perfect, would have + V3 (giả tưởng quá khứ)
- **Lưu ý**: Type 2 dùng "were" cho mọi ngôi với "to be"
- **Unless** = "If not": I won't go unless you come.
