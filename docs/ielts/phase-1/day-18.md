# Ngày 18 – Reported Speech

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Reported Statements

#### Cấu trúc cơ bản
```
He said that + [câu khẳng định]
He told me that + [câu khẳng định]
```

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|--------------|----------------|
| "I am happy." | He said that he was happy. |
| "I work here." | She said that she worked there. |
| "I saw him yesterday." | He said that he had seen him the day before. |
| "I will come." | She said that she would come. |
| "I can do it." | He said that he could do it. |
| "I must go." | She said that she had to go. |
| "I have finished." | He said that he had finished. |

### Thay đổi từ khi chuyển sang Reported Speech

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|--------------|----------------|
| now | then / at that time |
| here | there |
| this | that |
| these | those |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| last week | the week before / the previous week |
| next week | the following week |
| ago | before |
| I / we / my / our | he / she / they / his / her / their |
| you | I / he / she / they |
| this week | that week |
| this morning | that morning |

### Thay đổi thì (Tense Backshift)

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|--------------|----------------|
| Present Simple | Past Simple |
| Present Continuous | Past Continuous |
| Past Simple | Past Perfect |
| Present Perfect | Past Perfect |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| must | had to |
| may | might |
| shall | should |

### Reported Questions

#### Câu hỏi Yes/No
```
"Are you happy?" → He asked me if/whether I was happy.
"Do you like coffee?" → She asked whether I liked coffee.
```

#### Câu hỏi Wh-
```
"Where do you live?" → He asked where I lived.
"What are you doing?" → She asked what I was doing.
"When did you arrive?" → He asked when I had arrived.
```

### Reported Commands/Requests
```
"Sit down." → He told me to sit down.
"Don't go." → She told me not to go.
"Please help me." → He asked me to help him.
"Can you open the door?" → She asked me to open the door.
```

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### A piece of news you heard
```
I would like to tell you about a piece of news that I heard recently.
Last week, I read in the newspaper that a new technology company
had been established in my city. The company, which specializes in
artificial intelligence, announced that they would create hundreds of jobs
for local residents.

When I heard this news, I was excited. I immediately called my
friend, who had been looking for a job for several months. I told him
about the opportunity and suggested that he should apply. He thanked me
and said that he would send his resume the following day.

A few days later, my friend called me and told me that he had been
invited for an interview. He said that the interview had gone well
and the interviewers had been impressed with his skills. I was very
happy for him.

Yesterday, I received another call from my friend. He told me that he
had been offered the job! He said that he would start working the
next month and that the salary was better than he had expected. He
also mentioned that the company provided excellent benefits and
opportunities for professional growth.

This piece of news was important to me because it helped my friend
find employment. It also showed that my city was developing and
creating more opportunities for young professionals. The success of
my friend made me feel proud and encouraged.

I also learned about the company's plans for the future. I read that
they intended to expand their operations to other cities in the coming
years. They announced that they would invest heavily in research and
development and hoped to become a leading company in the AI industry.

This news has made me optimistic about the future of my city.
I believe that more companies will choose to establish their operations
here, which will bring more jobs and economic growth. It's exciting
to see positive developments in our community.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: A Piece of News You Heard

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What piece of news did you hear recently?
2. Where did you hear this news?
3. What did you do when you heard the news?
4. Did you share this news with anyone?
5. Why was this news important or interesting?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I would like to tell you about a piece of news that I heard..."

2. **What was the news** (45 giây)
   - "I read/heard that..."
   - "The news was about..."

3. **Reaction** (30 giây)
   - "When I heard this, I..."
   - "I immediately..."

4. **Actions taken** (45 giây)
   - "I told my friend that..."
   - "She said that..."

5. **Outcome & conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "The result was..."
   - "This news was important because..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I would like to tell you about a piece of news that I heard recently.
Last month, I read in a news article that my university had
announced plans to build a new library on campus. The news reported
that the library would be modern and equipped with the latest technology.

When I read this news, I was very excited. The old library on our
campus is quite small and doesn't have enough resources for all
students. I immediately shared this information with my classmates.
I told them that the new library would have more books, computers, and
study spaces.

My classmates reacted positively to the news. They said that the new
library would help students study more effectively. One of my friends,
who is interested in architecture, mentioned that he had seen the design
plans and that the building would be beautiful and environmentally
friendly.

A few days later, I heard more details about the project. The university
announced that construction would begin the following year and that the
library would be completed within two years. They also revealed that
the library would be open 24 hours a day, which would be very
convenient for students who study late at night.

This news was important to me for several reasons. First, it showed that
the university was investing in student education and facilities. Second,
having access to a modern library would greatly benefit my studies.
I often struggle to find resources I need, and the new library would
solve this problem.

I also learned that the library would offer various services such as
digital resources, online databases, and multimedia materials. They
announced that there would be quiet study rooms, group discussion areas,
and even a café where students could relax between study sessions.

This news has made me more enthusiastic about my studies. I feel that
the university values its students and is constantly improving. The prospect
of studying in a modern library motivates me to work harder and take
full advantage of the resources available to me.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Statements**: said/told that + câu
- **Questions**: asked if/whether (Yes/No), asked wh- (Wh- questions)
- **Commands**: told/asked + to + V
- **Thay đổi thì**: tense backshift (Present → Past, will → would)
- **Thay đổi từ**: now→then, here→there, today→that day
