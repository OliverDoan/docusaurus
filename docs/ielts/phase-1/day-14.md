# Ngày 14 – Review Tuần 1-2

## 📚 Tóm tắt các thì đã học

### Present Tenses
| Thì | Cấu trúc | Dùng khi |
|------|----------|----------|
| Present Simple | S + V(s/es) | Thói quen, sự thật, lịch trình |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + V-ing | Đang diễn ra, tạm thời, kế hoạch gần |
| Present Perfect | have/has + V3 | Kinh nghiệm, kết quả đến hiện tại, kéo dài đến hiện tại |
| Present Perfect Continuous | have/has been + V-ing | Nhấn thời lượng, quá trình tiếp tục |

### Past Tenses
| Thì | Cấu trúc | Dùng khi |
|------|----------|----------|
| Past Simple | S + V2 | Đã hoàn tất, có thời gian cụ thể |
| Past Continuous | was/were + V-ing | Đang diễn ra tại mốc quá khứ |
| Past Perfect | had + V3 | Hoàn tất trước hành động quá khứ khác |
| Past Perfect Continuous | had been + V-ing | Tiếp diễn đến mốc quá khứ |

### Future Tenses
| Thì | Cấu trúc | Dùng khi |
|------|----------|----------|
| Will | will + V | Quyết định tức thời, dự đoán, hứa hẹn |
| Be going to | am/is/are going to + V | Kế hoạch đã có, dự đoán có bằng chứng |
| Future Continuous | will be + V-ing | Đang diễn ra tại mốc tương lai |
| Future Perfect | will have + V3 | Hoàn tất trước mốc tương lai |

## 📖 Từ vựng tổng hợp

| Từ loại | Từ vựng |
|---------|----------|
| **Thời gian** | yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020, recently, lately, tomorrow, next week, by [date] |
| **Kinh nghiệm** | experience, achieve, accomplish, succeed, challenge, opportunity, milestone |
| **Hoạt động** | action, activity, behavior, habit, routine, practice, perform, execute |
| **Cảm xúc** | feel, emotion, mood, attitude,心态, mindset, perspective |
| **Định hướng** | plan, goal, objective, aim, purpose, intention, target |
| **Thành tích** | achievement, success, accomplishment, victory, triumph, attainment |

## 💬 Các mẫu câu quan trọng

### Tường thuật cuộc sống hàng ngày
```
I usually wake up at 6 AM. (Present Simple)
Right now, I am studying English. (Present Continuous)
I have studied English for 2 years. (Present Perfect)
I have been working on this project since morning. (Present Perfect Continuous)
```

### Tường thuật câu chuyện
```
I went to the beach yesterday. (Past Simple)
It was raining when I arrived. (Past Continuous)
I had never visited that place before. (Past Perfect)
I had been waiting for an hour when the bus arrived. (Past Perfect Continuous)
```

### Tường thuật tương lai
```
I will visit my parents tomorrow. (Future Simple)
I am going to take a trip next month. (Be going to)
At this time tomorrow, I will be flying to Paris. (Future Continuous)
By next year, I will have graduated. (Future Perfect)
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: 10 phút liên tục

### Câu hỏi gợi ý (ngẫu nhiên):
1. What is your daily routine?
2. What are you doing these days?
3. What have you achieved recently?
4. What happened last weekend?
5. What are your plans for next week?
6. Where will you be in 5 years?
7. What will you have achieved by age 30?
8. Compare your hometown with another city.
9. What are your hobbies?
10. What advice would you give to stay healthy?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Daily life (2 phút)** - dùng Present tenses
   - Routine, current activities, recent progress

2. **Past experiences (3 phút)** - dùng Past tenses
   - What happened recently, memorable events

3. **Future plans (3 phút)** - dùng Future tenses
   - Short-term and long-term goals

4. **Comparisons & opinions (2 phút)** - dùng so sánh, modal verbs
   - Compare places, give advice

### Sample Answer Overview:
```
I usually wake up at 6:30 every morning and start my day with a cup of coffee.
Currently, I am preparing for my IELTS exam, which has been taking most of
my time these past few months. I have been studying English for about two
years and have made significant progress.

Last weekend, something interesting happened to me. I was walking in the park
when I met an old friend that I hadn't seen for years. We had a great time
catching up and reminiscing about our school days. It reminded me of how
important friendships are.

In the near future, I plan to take the IELTS exam and hopefully get a good
score. After that, I am going to apply for a master's program abroad. In five
years, I will be working as a professional in my field, and I hope to have
established a successful career by then.

Comparing my hometown with where I live now, my hometown is smaller and
more peaceful than this city. However, the city offers more opportunities
and excitement. I should probably try to maintain a better work-life balance
because I have been working too much lately.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Present**: Simple (thói quen), Continuous (đang diễn ra), Perfect (kết quả hiện tại)
- **Past**: Simple (hoàn tất), Continuous (đang lúc đó), Perfect (trước hành động khác)
- **Future**: Simple (quyết định/duy đoán), Continuous (đang tương lai), Perfect (hoàn trước mốc)
- Luôn nhớ: thì + ngữ cảnh thích hợp
- Speaking: kết hợp nhiều thì một cách tự nhiên
