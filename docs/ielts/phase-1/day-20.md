# Ngày 20 – Complex Sentences

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Câu phức với từ nối

#### Because / Since / As (Vì)
```
I stayed home because it was raining.
Since I was tired, I went to bed.
As it was late, we left.
```

#### Although / Though / Even though (Mặc dù)
```
Although it was raining, we went out.
Though tired, she continued working.
Even though it's expensive, I'll buy it.
```

#### While / Whereas (Trong khi / Trong khi đó)
```
I cooked dinner while my brother was studying.
While I was walking, I saw a bird.
```

#### If (Nếu) - Conditionals
```
If it rains, I'll stay home.
If I had money, I would buy a car.
If I had known, I would have helped.
```

### So... that / Such... that (Quá... đến mức)

#### So + adj/adv + that
```
It's so hot that I can't work.
He was so tired that he fell asleep immediately.
The movie was so boring that I left early.
```

#### Such + (a/an) + noun + that
```
It was such a beautiful day that we went to the park.
She is such a kind person that everyone likes her.
They made such progress that the teacher was impressed.
```

### Too... to / Enough... to (Quá / Đủ)

#### Too + adj/adv + to + V
```
It's too hot to go outside.
The box is too heavy to lift.
He's too young to drive.
```

#### Adj/adv + enough + to + V
```
It's warm enough to go outside.
The box is light enough to lift.
He's old enough to drive.
```

### Neither... nor / Either... or (Cả... đều / Hoặc... hoặc)

#### Neither A nor B
```
Neither John nor Mary came to the party.
I neither like coffee nor tea.
```

#### Either A or B
```
Either you or your brother made this mistake.
I can either go by bus or by train.
```

### Both... and (Vừa... vừa...)

```
Both my parents and my friends support me.
He is both intelligent and hardworking.
```

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Environmental problems
```
I would like to talk about environmental problems, which have become
one of the most serious issues that our world faces today. There are
many different types of environmental pollution that threaten our
planet.

Air pollution is a major problem in many cities, especially in
developing countries where industrial activities are rapidly increasing.
Because factories and vehicles release harmful gases into the atmosphere,
air quality has deteriorated significantly. Consequently, many people
suffer from respiratory problems and other health issues. While some
governments have implemented regulations to control emissions, much more
needs to be done.

Water pollution is another serious concern. Industrial waste and
agricultural runoff contaminate rivers, lakes, and oceans. Although
water is essential for all living things, many people around the
world still don't have access to clean drinking water. Moreover,
marine life is being destroyed by plastic waste that ends up in the
oceans. So much plastic is being produced that it has become a
major environmental crisis.

Climate change is perhaps the most pressing environmental problem.
Because carbon emissions from burning fossil fuels have increased
dramatically over the past century, global temperatures have risen
significantly. As a result, we are witnessing more extreme weather
events such as hurricanes, floods, and droughts. Although some people
still deny the reality of climate change, scientific evidence is
overwhelming.

Deforestation is also contributing to environmental degradation.
Since forests absorb carbon dioxide and provide habitats for countless
species, their destruction has severe consequences. While forests are
being cut down for agriculture and urban development, we are losing
biodiversity at an alarming rate. Many species are becoming
endangered because their natural habitats are disappearing.

There are solutions to these environmental problems. Governments need
to implement stricter regulations on industrial emissions. Furthermore,
individuals can make a difference by reducing, reusing, and recycling.
We should also support renewable energy sources such as solar and
wind power. Although transitioning to clean energy requires investment,
the long-term benefits are worth it.

In conclusion, environmental problems are complex and require urgent
action. Because our future depends on the health of our planet,
we must take responsibility for protecting the environment. By working
together and making sustainable choices, we can create a better
future for ourselves and generations to come.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Environmental Problems

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What are the main environmental problems in your country?
2. What causes pollution?
3. What are the effects of climate change?
4. What can individuals do to help the environment?
5. What should governments do?
6. Do you think environmental problems are getting worse?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I would like to talk about environmental problems..."

2. **Problem 1** (45 giây)
   - "One major problem is..."
   - "Because..."

3. **Problem 2** (45 giây)
   - "Another concern is..."
   - "Although..."

4. **Solutions** (45 giây)
   - "There are several solutions."
   - "Governments should..."

5. **Conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "In conclusion..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I would like to talk about environmental problems, which are serious
issues that affect our planet. There are several major environmental
concerns that need urgent attention.

Air pollution is one of the most serious problems in my country,
especially in large cities. Because many people use motorcycles and
cars, air quality has deteriorated significantly. Consequently,
residents in urban areas often suffer from health problems related
to pollution. Although the government has implemented some
regulations, much more needs to be done to improve air quality.

Water pollution is another significant issue. Many rivers and lakes
in our country are contaminated because industrial waste is
dumped into them without proper treatment. Moreover, agricultural
runoff containing pesticides and fertilizers also pollutes water
sources. As a result, many people don't have access to
clean drinking water, especially in rural areas.

Plastic waste has become a major environmental crisis as well.
Because single-use plastics are so convenient, people use them
excessively. However, these plastics take hundreds of years to
decompose. So much plastic ends up in our oceans that marine
life is being severely affected. Fish and other sea creatures
mistake plastic pieces for food, which can be fatal.

Climate change is also having an impact on our country. We are
experiencing more extreme weather events such as stronger typhoons
and unpredictable rainfall patterns. Since Vietnam has a long
coastline, rising sea levels pose a significant threat to coastal
communities. While we cannot stop climate change completely,
we can take actions to reduce its impact.

There are several solutions to these environmental problems.
Governments should implement stricter environmental regulations and
invest more in renewable energy. Furthermore, individuals can make
a difference by reducing their carbon footprint. We can use public
transportation, reduce energy consumption, and recycle. Education is
also crucial because people need to understand the importance of
environmental protection.

In conclusion, environmental problems require urgent action from both
governments and individuals. By working together and making
sustainable choices, we can protect our planet for future
generations.
```
