# Ngày 13 – Gerund & Infinitive

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Gerund (V-ing)

#### Cấu trúc
- V-ing đóng vai trò như danh từ hoặc động từ

#### Khi nào dùng Gerund?
1. Làm chủ ngữ hoặc tân ngữ
   - Swimming is fun.
   - I enjoy reading.

2. Sau các động từ sau:
   - **enjoy**: I enjoy swimming.
   - **like**: I like dancing.
   - **love**: She loves singing.
   - **hate**: He hates working.
   - **prefer**: I prefer staying home.
   - **mind**: Do you mind waiting?
   - **suggest**: I suggest going home.
   - **recommend**: I recommend studying hard.
   - **practice**: I practice speaking daily.
   - **avoid**: I avoid eating late.
   - **finish**: I finished working.
   - **keep**: Keep trying!
   - **start**: Start learning today.
   - **stop**: Stop smoking.
   - **imagine**: Imagine living there.
   - **miss**: I miss seeing you.
   - **consider**: I considered changing jobs.
   - **discuss**: We discussed the problem.
   - **understand**: I understand feeling that way.

3. Sau giới từ (in, on, at, for, of, about, without)
   - I'm interested in learning.
   - Thank you for helping.
   - She is good at cooking.

### Infinitive (to V)

#### Cấu trúc
- to + V (nguyên thể)

#### Khi nào dùng Infinitive?
1. Sau các động từ sau:
   - **want**: I want to go.
   - **need**: She needs to study.
   - **hope**: I hope to see you.
   - **plan**: We plan to visit.
   - **decide**: He decided to leave.
   - **expect**: I expect to pass.
   - **promise**: She promised to help.
   - **agree**: I agree to help.
   - **refuse**: He refused to go.
   - **offer**: I offered to drive.
   - **manage**: She managed to finish.
   - **afford**: I can't afford to buy.
   - **fail**: He failed to pass.
   - **pretend**: She pretended to be happy.
   - **seem**: He seems to be busy.
   - **appear**: She appears to know.
   - **tend**: I tend to worry.
   - **happen**: I happened to meet him.
   - **learn**: She learned to swim.

2. Sau các tính từ:
   - **easy**: It's easy to learn.
   - **hard**: It's hard to do.
   - **difficult**: It's difficult to understand.
   - **important**: It's important to save.
   - **necessary**: It's necessary to eat.
   - **possible**: It's possible to finish.
   - **impossible**: It's impossible to see.
   - **dangerous**: It's dangerous to swim.
   - **safe**: It's safe to walk.

3. Để chỉ mục đích:
   - I study to get a good job.
   - She works to support her family.

4. Sau cấu trúc **too + adj** và **adj + enough**:
   - too tired to walk
   - old enough to drive

### Gerund hoặc Infinitive (khác nhau)

| Động từ + Gerund | Động từ + Infinitive |
|------------------|---------------------|
| remember doing (đã làm) | remember to do (nhớ phải làm) |
| stop doing (dừng hành động) | stop to do (dừng để làm việc khác) |
| try doing (thử cách) | try to do (cố gắng) |
| forget doing (đã làm quên) | forget to do (quên làm) |
| mean doing (hàm ý) | mean to do (có ý định) |
| go on doing (tiếp tục) | go on to do (chuyển sang việc khác) |

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Hobbies and interests
```
I have several hobbies that I enjoy in my free time. My favorite hobby is
reading. I love reading books of different genres, especially fiction and
history. Reading helps me relax and learn new things at the same time. I
usually read for an hour before going to bed.

Another hobby of mine is photography. I enjoy taking photos of nature and
cityscapes. Photography allows me to capture beautiful moments and memories.
I also like editing photos and sharing them with my friends. It's a creative
hobby that helps me see the world from different perspectives.

I also enjoy playing sports. I like playing badminton and swimming. Playing
sports is not only fun but also good for my health. It helps me stay active
and relieve stress. I usually play badminton with my friends on weekends and
go swimming a few times a week.

Cooking is another interest of mine. I learned to cook from my mother, and
I've been improving my skills over the years. I enjoy trying new recipes and
experimenting with different ingredients. Cooking for my family gives me
pleasure, especially when they enjoy the food I prepare.

I'm also interested in learning languages. I enjoy learning English and have
recently started learning a bit of French. Learning a language is challenging
but rewarding. It opens up new opportunities and helps me connect with people
from different cultures.

In my free time, I also like watching movies and listening to music. These
activities help me relax after a long day at work. I prefer action movies and
classical music, but I'm open to trying different genres.

My hobbies give me a balanced life. They help me relax, learn new things,
stay healthy, and connect with others. I believe that having interesting
hobbies is important for personal growth and happiness.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Hobbies and Interests

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What are your hobbies?
2. What do you do in your free time?
3. Why do you enjoy these activities?
4. How much time do you spend on your hobbies?
5. Did you have different hobbies when you were younger?
6. How do your hobbies help you?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I have several hobbies that I enjoy."

2. **Main hobby** (45 giây)
   - "My favorite hobby is..."
   - "I enjoy V-ing because..."

3. **Other hobbies** (45 giây)
   - "I also like V-ing..."
   - "Another hobby of mine is..."

4. **Time spent** (30 giây)
   - "I spend... [time] on..."
   - "I usually [activity] on..."

5. **Benefits & conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "These hobbies help me..."
   - "Overall, my hobbies give me..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I have several hobbies that I enjoy doing in my free time. My favorite
hobby is reading. I love reading books, especially novels and biographies.
Reading helps me relax and escape from daily stress. It also expands my
knowledge and vocabulary. I usually read for about an hour every day,
usually before going to bed.

Another hobby of mine is playing the guitar. I started learning when I
was in high school and have been practicing ever since. Playing music is
a great way for me to express myself and relieve stress. I enjoy playing
both classical and contemporary songs. I try to practice at least three
times a week.

I also enjoy photography. I like taking photos of nature, landscapes, and
city scenes. Photography allows me to capture beautiful moments and preserve
memories. It's also a creative hobby that helps me see the world from
different perspectives. I often go on photo walks on weekends.

Cooking is another interest of mine. I learned to cook from my mother and
have been improving my skills over the years. I enjoy trying new recipes
and experimenting with different ingredients. Cooking for my family and
friends gives me satisfaction, especially when they enjoy the food I make.

In my free time, I also like swimming and watching movies. Swimming is good
exercise and helps me stay fit. Watching movies, especially comedies and
thrillers, is a relaxing way to end the day.

These hobbies are important to me for several reasons. They help me relax
after work, learn new things, stay healthy, and connect with others.
Having interesting hobbies makes my life more fulfilling and enjoyable.
I believe everyone should have hobbies that bring them joy.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

### Bài tập 1: Điền Gerund hoặc Infinitive
1. I enjoy ______ (read) books.
2. She wants ______ (go) to the movies.
3. He decided ______ (leave) early.
4. We like ______ (play) football.
5. I hope ______ (see) you soon.

### Bài tập 2: Điền giới từ + Gerund hoặc to + Infinitive
1. I'm interested ______ (learn) English.
2. Thank you ______ (help) me.
3. She is good ______ (play) the piano.
4. I decided ______ (study) abroad.
5. He agreed ______ (meet) us.

### Bài tập 3: Chọn dạng đúng
1. I remember (to meet/meeting) him yesterday.
2. Please remember (to lock/locking) the door.
3. She stopped (to smoke/smoking) last year.
4. We stopped (to buy/buying) some food.
5. He tried (to fix/fixing) the problem.

### Bài tập 4: Viết về sở thích
Viết 5 câu mô tả sở thích của bạn, dùng Gerund hoặc Infinitive đúng cách.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Gerund (V-ing)**: enjoy, like, love, hate, prefer, mind, suggest
- **Infinitive (to V)**: want, need, hope, plan, decide, expect, promise
- **Sau giới từ**: phải dùng Gerund
- **Sau tính từ**: thường dùng Infinitive
- **Lưu ý**: remember/stop/try/forget với Gerund/Infinitive khác nghĩa
