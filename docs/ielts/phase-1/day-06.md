# Ngày 6 – Present Perfect Continuous

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + have/has + been + V-ing
- **Phủ định**: S + have/has + not + been + V-ing
- **Nghi vấn**: Have/Has + S + been + V-ing?

### Phân biệt Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

| Present Perfect | Present Perfect Continuous |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| I have written 3 letters. (kết quả) | I have been writing letters. (quá trình) |
| She has painted the room. (hoàn thành) | She has been painting all day. (còn đang làm) |
| He has worked here 5 years. (thực tế) | He has been working here 5 years. (nhấn mạnh thời lượng) |

### Khi nào dùng Present Perfect Continuous?
1. **Hành động bắt đầu trong quá khứ, kéo dài đến hiện tại, và có thể tiếp tục**
   - I have been studying English for 3 years. (Vẫn đang học)
   - She has been working at this company since 2020. (Vẫn đang làm)

2. **Nhấn mạnh vào thời lượng của hành động**
   - They have been waiting for 2 hours. (Nhấn vào thời gian chờ)
   - I have been reading this book all day.

3. **Hành động vừa mới kết thúc nhưng kết quả hiển nhiên**
   - I have been running. (Tôi đang thở dốc)
   - She has been crying. (Mắt cô ấy đỏ)

### Sự khác biệt với Present Perfect

**Dùng Present Perfect Continuous khi:**
- Nhấn mạnh vào **thời lượng** hoặc **quá trình**
- Hành động có thể **tiếp tục**
- Với động từ liên tục: study, work, live, wait

**Dùng Present Perfect khi:**
- Nhấn mạnh vào **kết quả** hoặc **số lượng**
- Hành động đã **hoàn thành**
- Với những động từ không chia tiếp diễn: know, love, like, understand

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### What I've been doing recently
```
I have been very busy lately. For the past few months, I have been preparing
for the IELTS exam. I have been studying English every day, practicing all
four skills: listening, reading, writing, and speaking. I have been taking
online courses and watching English videos to improve my vocabulary.

At work, I have been working on several important projects. I have been
collaborating with different teams and learning new technologies. The work
has been challenging, but I have been enjoying the challenge.

In my free time, I have been trying to maintain a healthy lifestyle. I have been
exercising regularly and eating healthier food. I have also been spending
more time with my family, which has been very relaxing.

Overall, I have been feeling productive and motivated. I believe that all the
effort I have been putting in will pay off in the future.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: What Have You Been Doing Recently?

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What have you been doing lately?
2. How long have you been studying English?
3. What have you been working on recently?
4. Have you been exercising regularly?
5. What have you been learning recently?
6. How have you been feeling these days?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I've been quite busy lately." / "Recently, I've been..."

2. **Main activity** (45 giây)
   - "I've been [V-ing] for..."
   - "For the past [time], I've been..."

3. **Other activities** (45 giây)
   - "I've also been..."
   - "Besides that, I've been..."

4. **Feelings & progress** (30 giây)
   - "I've been feeling..." / "I've been making progress..."

5. **Future outlook** (30 giây)
   - "I plan to continue..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I've been quite busy lately. For the past few months, I've been focusing on
improving my English skills. I've been studying English for about two hours
every day. I've been practicing speaking with language partners online and
watching English movies to improve my listening. I've also been reading books
and articles in English to expand my vocabulary.

At work, I've been working on several important projects. I've been developing
a new software application, which has been both challenging and interesting.
I've been collaborating with colleagues from different departments, and I've
been learning a lot from them. The project has been progressing well, and
I've been feeling confident about our results.

In my personal life, I've been trying to maintain a work-life balance. I've been
spending more quality time with my family on weekends. I've also been
exercising regularly - I've been going to the gym three times a week. This has
been helping me stay healthy and energetic.

Overall, I've been feeling motivated and productive. I've been making good
progress in all areas of my life, and I plan to continue working hard to achieve
my goals.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- Cấu trúc: have/has + been + V-ing
- Nhấn mạnh: thời lượng, quá trình, hành động có thể tiếp tục
- Dùng with: for (khoảng thời gian), since (mốc thời gian)
- Không dùng với: know, understand, like, love (state verbs)
- Khác với Present Perfect: nhấn quá trình hơn kết quả
