# Ngày 16 – Passive Voice

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc cơ bản
```
Object + be + V3 + (by + Agent)
```

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|-------|--------|----------|
| Present Simple | They make cars. | Cars are made. |
| Present Continuous | They are making cars. | Cars are being made. |
| Past Simple | They made cars. | Cars were made. |
| Past Continuous | They were making cars. | Cars were being made. |
| Present Perfect | They have made cars. | Cars have been made. |
| Past Perfect | They had made cars. | Cars had been made. |
| Future Simple | They will make cars. | Cars will be made. |
| Future Perfect | They will have made cars. | Cars will have been made. |

### Khi nào dùng Passive Voice?

1. **Không biết hoặc không quan trọng ai là người thực hiện**
   - My car was stolen. (Không biết ai lấy)
   - Rice is grown in many countries. (Không quan trọng ai trồng)

2. **Nhấn mạnh vào đối tượng hơn người thực hiện**
   - Mona Lisa was painted by Da Vinci. (Nhấn vào bức tranh)
   - The letter was sent yesterday. (Nhấn vào bức thư)

3. **Sự báo cáo, tin tức, khoa học**
   - The experiment was conducted successfully.
   - The decision was made by the committee.

4. **Khi người thực hiện rõ ràng**
   - This house was built by my grandfather.
   - The document was signed by the manager.

### Động từ có cấu trúc đặc biệt trong Passive

#### Câu khẳng định
- They gave me a gift. → I was given a gift.
- The company offers him a job. → He is offered a job.

#### Câu nghi vấn
- Who wrote this book? → Who was this book written by?
- What did they say? → What was said?

#### Câu phủ định
- They didn't fix the problem. → The problem wasn't fixed.
- She hasn't received the letter. → The letter hasn't been received.

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Technology in daily life
```
Technology has changed our lives in many ways. The internet, which was
invented in the 1960s, has transformed how we communicate and access

information. Today, billions of websites are accessed by people around the
world every day.

Smartphones are used by almost everyone nowadays. These devices have become
essential tools in our daily lives. Messages are sent instantly, photos are
shared with friends, and information is accessed at our fingertips. The
first smartphone was introduced by IBM in 1992, but modern smartphones
were popularized by Apple.

Artificial Intelligence is being developed rapidly in recent years. Many tasks
that used to be done by humans are now performed by AI systems. Customer
service inquiries are answered by chatbots, and product recommendations are
made by algorithms based on our preferences.

In transportation, electric vehicles are being produced to reduce pollution.
Traditional cars are being replaced by more environmentally friendly options.
Many cities are planning to ban gasoline-powered cars in the future.
Public transportation is being improved with smart technologies that help
passengers reach their destinations more efficiently.

Education has also been transformed by technology. Online courses are offered
by many universities, and students can learn from anywhere. Information is
available on the internet, and research can be done without visiting
libraries. During the pandemic, classes were conducted online to ensure
safety.

In our homes, many devices are controlled by automation. Lights can be
turned on and off by voice commands, and temperature is adjusted
automatically. Security systems are monitored by cameras, and energy
consumption is optimized by smart devices.

While technology brings many benefits, some challenges also need to be
addressed. Privacy is a concern for many people, and job losses are
feared by workers as automation increases. However, with proper regulation
and adaptation, technology will continue to improve our lives.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Technology in Daily Life

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. How has technology changed your daily life?
2. What technology do you use most often?
3. Do you think technology has made life better? Why?
4. What are the positive and negative effects of technology?
5. How will technology change in the future?
6. What technology would you like to see developed?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "Technology has significantly changed my daily life."

2. **Communication** (40 giây)
   - "Communication is now..."
   - "Messages are sent..."

3. **Work & education** (40 giây)
   - "Work and education are..."
   - "Information is accessed..."

4. **Benefits** (30 giây)
   - "The main benefits are..."
   - "Tasks are made easier by..."

5. **Concerns & conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "However, some concerns exist..."
   - "Overall, technology has..."

### Sample Answer:
```
Technology has significantly changed my daily life in numerous ways.
Communication, in particular, has been revolutionized. Messages are sent
instantly through messaging apps, and video calls are made with people
around the world. Distance is no longer a barrier because people are
connected by the internet.

Work and education are also impacted by technology. Documents are shared
electronically, and meetings are conducted online. Information is accessed
through search engines, and research can be done from anywhere. During
the pandemic, work and classes were done from home, which wouldn't have
been possible without technology.

In daily routines, many tasks are simplified by smart devices. Shopping is
done online, and groceries are delivered to our homes. Transportation
is arranged through ride-hailing apps, and navigation is provided by GPS.
Time is saved on many activities because processes are automated by technology.

However, some negative effects are noticed. Privacy is a concern for many
people as personal data is collected by companies. Social skills are sometimes
neglected because people are distracted by their devices. Face-to-face
interactions are reduced as communication is shifted to digital platforms.

Despite these concerns, I believe technology has made life better overall.
Efficiency is improved, and convenience is increased by modern innovations.
As long as technology is used responsibly and in moderation, the benefits
will outweigh the drawbacks.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Cấu trúc**: Object + be + V3 (+ by Agent)
- **Quy tắc**: chia be theo thì của câu active
- **Dùng khi**: không biết ai làm, nhấn đối tượng, báo cáo khoa học
- **Lưu ý**: động từ đặc biệt với 2 tân ngữ
- **Not**: Không dùng với động từ không có đối tượng (happen, occur)
