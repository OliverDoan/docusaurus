# Ngày 30 – Final Mock Test (IELTS Speaking Part 1-2-3)

## 📚 IELTS Speaking Complete Structure

| Part | Thời gian | Nội dung chính |
|------|----------|----------------|
| Part 1 | 4-5 phút | Câu hỏi về quê hương, sở thích, công việc, học tập |
| Part 2 | 3-4 phút chuẩn bị | Mô tả một chủ đề cụ thể (1 phút chuẩn bị + 2 phút nói + 1 phút câu hỏi) |
| Part 3 | 4-5 phút | Thảo luận sâu hơn về chủ đề Part 2 |

## IELTS Speaking Part 1 – Complete Mock

### Sample Questions & Ideal Answers

**Q1: What is your hometown like?**
```
My hometown is [city name], which is located in [country/region].
It's a [size: large/small/medium-sized] city with a rich
[cultural/historical] heritage. The [special feature: Old Quarter,
downtown area, countryside] is particularly [adjective: charming,
beautiful, peaceful].

What I love most about my hometown is [what you love: the
people, the food, the atmosphere]. The locals are [adjective:
friendly, welcoming, hospitable], and the [local dish] is
[adjective: delicious, famous]. It's a place that holds many
[emotion: happy, childhood, nostalgic] memories for me.
```

**Q2: Do you work or are you a student?**
```
I'm currently working as a [profession: software developer, teacher,
engineer, doctor]. I've been in this position for [time period],
and I find it both [adjectives: challenging, rewarding,
interesting]. My main responsibilities involve [brief description
of work].

Before this job, I studied [subject/field] at [university]. My
education has been very helpful in my current role because [how
education helped]. I'm grateful for the opportunities that
my career has provided me so far.
```

**Q3: What do you enjoy doing in your free time?**
```
In my free time, I enjoy [main hobby: reading, playing sports,
painting, cooking]. I've been interested in this since [time
period], and it has become an important part of my life.

Besides [main hobby], I also like to spend time with [family/
friends]. We often [activity: go to restaurants, watch movies,
play games]. These social interactions are important to me because
[why they're important].

I also try to maintain a healthy lifestyle by [health activity:
exercising, cooking, meditating]. This helps me stay
[adjective: balanced, energetic, focused] and ready for my
work and other responsibilities.
```

**Q4: Do you prefer living in a city or in the countryside?**
```
I prefer living in a [city/countryside] for several reasons.
First, [reason 1: convenience, peace, access to nature] is
important to me. Living in a [preferred location] allows me to
[benefit 1].

However, I can also appreciate the [other location] for
[reason 2: the quiet atmosphere, the opportunities]. Sometimes
I visit the [other location] to [activity] and enjoy its
[adjective: peaceful, exciting, diverse] environment.

Overall, while both places have their advantages, I feel more
comfortable and suited in a [preferred location] because
[final reason for preference].
```

## IELTS Speaking Part 2 – Complete Mock

### Sample Topic: Describe a person you admire

**Task card:**
```
Describe a person you admire.

You should say:
- Who this person is
- How long you have known them
- What they do
- Explain why you admire this person
```

**Sample Answer (2 minutes):**
```
I would like to talk about my [relationship: father, mother,
teacher, friend, public figure], who is [name] and someone I
greatly admire. I have known this person for [time period], and
they have had a significant influence on my life.

[Name] is a [profession/description of what they do]. They have
been working in this field for [time period], and they are
[adjectives: dedicated, passionate, successful] in what they
do. I have watched them [what they do professionally] and have
always been impressed by their [quality: work ethic, knowledge,
skill].

What I admire most about this person is their [quality 1: kindness,
intelligence, perseverance, leadership]. I have seen many
examples of how they demonstrate this quality. For instance, [specific
example of the quality]. This quality has [effect: inspired me,
helped others, achieved great things].

Another characteristic that I respect is their [quality 2: generosity,
honesty, creativity]. They always [how they demonstrate this
quality], even when [challenging circumstances]. This shows
their strength of character and [virtue: integrity, compassion,
courage].

The influence that [name] has had on me has been profound.
They have taught me the importance of [lesson 1: hard work,
kindness, education]. Their [guidance/support/advice] has helped me
through [challenging situation].

I hope that one day I can become [what you hope to be like them].
They are not only a [relationship] but also a [role model/
mentor] who has shaped my values and aspirations. That's why I
admire them so much.
```

## IELTS Speaking Part 3 – Complete Mock

### Sample Questions Based on Part 2 Topic

**Q1: Why do people admire others?**
```
I believe people admire others for various reasons. First, we often
look up to those who have achieved great success in their fields.
People who excel in [areas: sports, arts, business, science] inspire
others to work hard and pursue their own goals.

Another reason is that we admire qualities like [qualities: courage,
kindness, intelligence, leadership]. When we see someone
demonstrating these traits consistently, it motivates us to develop
similar characteristics in ourselves.

We also admire people who overcome difficulties and adversity.
Seeing someone face challenges with [quality: resilience, determination,
optimism] gives us hope and encouragement. It shows that
obstacles can be overcome with the right attitude.

Additionally, people often admire those who contribute positively
to society. Individuals who [contribution: help others, create
art, make scientific discoveries] earn our respect because they
use their talents for the greater good.
```

**Q2: Do you think admiration can change over time?**
```
Yes, I believe that who we admire can definitely change over
time. This happens for several reasons related to our personal
growth and changing perspectives.

As we grow and gain more life experience, our understanding of
what makes someone admirable evolves. When I was younger, I
admired people for [superficial qualities: fame, wealth, physical
appearance]. However, as I've matured, I now value [deeper
qualities: character, wisdom, kindness, integrity] much more.

Our circumstances and needs also influence who we admire. For
example, when I was starting my career, I looked up to [type of
person: successful professionals in my field]. As I've
advanced, I now appreciate [different type of person: mentors,
wise leaders, people with balance].

Life experiences also change our admiration. After going through
[personal challenges], I've developed greater respect for people
who have overcome similar struggles. I now admire resilience and
perseverance more than I did before.

Furthermore, as we learn more about the world, we discover
new role models who inspire us. Education, travel, and exposure
to different perspectives introduce us to people we might never
have admired otherwise.
```

**Q3: How do people in your country show admiration?**
```
In my country, people show admiration in various ways, both
traditional and modern. One common way is through [expression:
praise and compliments]. We often express our appreciation
directly by telling people what we admire about them.

Another traditional method involves [action: showing respect through
behavior]. In our culture, we demonstrate admiration by
[behaviors: listening to elders, respecting teachers, following
leaders' guidance]. This shows that we value their experience
and wisdom.

People also show admiration through [supportive actions:
helping, supporting, celebrating with others]. When someone
achieves something significant, family, friends, and colleagues
often [celebrate: organize celebrations, give gifts, public
recognition] to acknowledge their accomplishment.

In modern times, social media has become a new platform for
expressing admiration. People share [content: photos, stories,
messages] to celebrate others' achievements and qualities.
This allows admiration to spread more widely and quickly.

However, I believe the most meaningful admiration is shown
through [actions: consistent support, being present during
challenges, long-term relationships]. These actions demonstrate
genuine appreciation rather than just momentary praise.
```

## 📊 Đánh giá Speaking IELTS

### Các tiêu chí đánh giá

| Tiêu chí | Điểm cao (7.0-9.0) |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Fluency & Coherence** | Nói liên tục, mạch lạc |
| **Lexical Resource** | Từ vựng đa dạng, dùng chính xác |
| **Grammatical Range** | Dùng nhiều thì, cấu trúc phức |
| **Pronunciation** | Phát âm rõ ràng, nhịp độ tốt |
| **Chia câu** | Câu dài vừa phải, đa dạng |
| **Từ nối** | Dùng từ nối tự nhiên |
| **Mở rộng** | Trả lời có mở rộng hợp lý |
| **Thời gian** | Đạt yêu cầu thời gian |

### Bảng kiểm tra tự đánh giá

| Tiêu chí | 1-2 | 3-4 | 5-6 | 7-9 |
|---------|------|------|------|------|
| Fluency | Nhiều dừng | Dừng đôi khi | Dừng ít | Ít quan |
| Từ vựng | Cơ bản | Đa dạng | Hiệu quả | Đa dạng, chính xác |
| Ngữ pháp | Lỗi nhiều | Một số lỗi | Lỗi ít | Ít quan |
| Phát âm | Khó hiểu | Có thể hiểu | Rõ ràng | Xuất sắc |

### Lịch bản cải thiện

**Theo từng tiêu chí:**
1. **Fluency thấp**: Luyện nói liên tục, ghi âm
2. **Từ vựng ít**: Học từ theo chủ đề, dùng từ hiếu
3. **Ngữ pháp yếu**: Ôn tập lại các thì, cấu trúc
4. **Phát âm kém**: Luyện nghe, bắt chước theo speaker bản xứ
