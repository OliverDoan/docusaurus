# Ngày 11 – Comparisons

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### So sánh hơn (Comparative)

#### Cấu trúc cơ bản
- Tính từ ngắn: S + be + adj-er + than + O
- Tính từ dài: S + be + more/less + adj + than + O

#### Quy tắc thêm -er
1. Thông thường: thêm **-er**
   - tall → taller, fast → faster, old → older

2. Tính từ kết thúc bằng **-e**: chỉ thêm **-r**
   - nice → nicer, large → larger

3. Tính từ kết thúc phụ âm + **-y**: đổi y thành **-ier**
   - happy → happier, heavy → heavier, busy → busier

4. Tính từ 1 âm tiết, kết thúc phụ âm + 1 phụ âm đơn: gấp đôi phụ âm + er
   - big → bigger, hot → hotter, thin → thinner

5. Tính từ bất quy tắc
   - good → better
   - bad → worse
   - far → farther/further

### So sánh nhất (Superlative)

#### Cấu trúc cơ bản
- Tính từ ngắn: S + be + the + adj-est + (danh từ)
- Tính từ dài: S + be + the + most/least + adj + (danh từ)

#### Quy tắc thêm -est
1. Thông thường: thêm **-est**
   - tall → the tallest, fast → the fastest

2. Kết thúc **-e**: chỉ thêm **-st**
   - nice → the nicest, large → the largest

3. Phụ âm + **-y**: đổi y thành **-iest**
   - happy → the happiest, heavy → the heaviest

4. Gấp đôi phụ âm: big → the biggest, hot → the hottest

5. Bất quy tắc
   - good → the best
   - bad → the worst
   - far → the farthest/furthest

### So sánh kép (Double Comparatives)

#### Cấu trúc 1: Càng... càng...
```
The + comparative ... , the + comparative ...
```
- The more you study, the more you learn.
- The harder you work, the better results you get.

#### Cấu trúc 2: Dần dần
```
Comparative + and + comparative
```
- Getting bigger and bigger.
- Becoming more and more expensive.

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Compare cities
```
I would like to compare Hanoi with Ho Chi Minh City, the two largest
cities in Vietnam. There are several similarities and differences between them.

In terms of size and population, Ho Chi Minh City is larger and more crowded
than Hanoi. HCM City has a population of over 10 million people, while Hanoi
has about 8 million. HCM City covers a larger area than Hanoi.

However, Hanoi is the capital and the political center of the country, which
makes it more important in terms of government. Hanoi has more historical
sites and traditional architecture than HCM City. The Old Quarter in
Hanoi is older and more charming than most areas in HCM City.

When it comes to modern development, HCM City is more modern and dynamic than
Hanoi. It has taller buildings, more shopping malls, and a more vibrant
nightlife. The pace of life in HCM City is faster and busier than in Hanoi.

In terms of transportation, HCM City has a more developed public transport
system, including the first metro line in Vietnam. However, Hanoi has a
better subway system under construction, which will make transportation more
convenient in the future.

Both cities are attractive in their own ways. Hanoi is more peaceful and
culturally rich, while HCM City is more exciting and economically dynamic.
The cost of living in HCM City is slightly higher than in Hanoi, especially
for housing.

Overall, both cities offer great opportunities for living and working. Which
city is better depends on personal preferences. If you prefer a quiet,
culturally rich environment, Hanoi might be better. If you enjoy a fast-paced,
modern lifestyle, HCM City would be more suitable.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Compare Your City with Another City

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. Compare your hometown with another city.
2. What are the similarities between these two cities?
3. What are the differences?
4. Which city do you prefer? Why?
5. How has your city changed over time?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I would like to compare [city A] with [city B]."

2. **Similarities** (40 giây)
   - "Both cities are..."
   - "They both have..."

3. **Differences - Feature 1** (40 giây)
   - "However, [city A] is... than [city B]."
   - "[city A] has more... than..."

4. **Differences - Feature 2** (40 giây)
   - "In terms of..."
   - "[city A] is more... while [city B] is..."

5. **Preference & conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "I prefer [city] because..."
   - "Overall, both cities are..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I would like to compare Hanoi, my hometown, with Da Nang, another major city
in Vietnam. These two cities have both similarities and differences.

Both cities are located in Vietnam and share some cultural characteristics.
They both have beautiful beaches nearby - Da Nang has its famous beaches,
while Hanoi has easy access to beaches like Sam Son. Both cities are
popular tourist destinations and offer delicious local cuisine.

However, there are significant differences between them. First, Hanoi is much
older and more historically significant than Da Nang. Hanoi has a history of
over a thousand years, while Da Nang is a relatively young city. Hanoi has
more ancient temples, pagodas, and historical sites than Da Nang.

In terms of size and population, Hanoi is larger and more crowded than Da Nang.
Hanoi has a population of about 8 million people, while Da Nang has only
around 1.2 million. Hanoi is busier and more dynamic than Da Nang, especially
in the city center.

Climate is another important difference. Da Nang has a more tropical climate
with warmer temperatures year-round. Hanoi experiences four distinct seasons,
including cold winters. The weather in Hanoi is more varied than in Da Nang.

When it comes to lifestyle, Da Nang is more peaceful and relaxed than Hanoi.
Life in Da Nang is slower and more laid-back, while Hanoi is always busy
and hectic. Da Nang is known for its clean and friendly environment, making
it more pleasant for living.

I prefer Hanoi because it's my hometown and I have many memories there. However,
I also love visiting Da Nang for its beautiful beaches and relaxed atmosphere.
Each city has its own charm and unique character.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **So sánh hơn**: adj-er/more + than
- **So sánh nhất**: the + adj-est/most
- **So sánh kép**: The + comparative..., the + comparative
- Quy tắc thêm -er/-est: -y → -ier/-iest, gấp đôi phụ âm
- Bất quy tắc: good/better/best, bad/worse/worst
