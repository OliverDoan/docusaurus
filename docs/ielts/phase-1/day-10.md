# Ngày 10 – Modal Verbs

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Danh sách Modal Verbs

| Modal | Nghĩa chính | Công thức |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| can | khả năng, cho phép | S + can + V |
| could | khả năng quá khứ, đề nghị lịch sự | S + could + V |
| may | khả năng, phép | S + may + V |
| might | khả năng nhỏ, không chắc chắn | S + might + V |
| must | bắt buộc, chắc chắn | S + (must/must not) + V |
| should | lời khuyên, nên | S + should + V |
| would | hy vọng, điều kiện, lịch sự | S + would + V |
| have to | phải (bắt buộc bên ngoài) | S + have/has to + V |
| ought to | nên (formal) | S + ought to + V |

### Can / Could

**Can**: Khả năng hiện tại, cho phép
- I can swim. (Tôi biết bơi)
- You can use my phone. (Bạn có thể dùng điện thoại của tôi)

**Could**:
1. Khả năng trong quá khứ
- I could swim when I was young. (Tôi đã biết bơi khi còn nhỏ)

2. Đề nghị lịch sự
- Could you help me? (Bạn giúp tôi được không?)

3. Khả năng trong tương lai (không chắc chắn)
- We could go to the beach tomorrow. (Có thể chúng ta sẽ đi biển)

### May / Might

**May**: Khả năng, phép
- It may rain tomorrow. (Có thể mưa)
- May I come in? (Tôi vào được không?)

**Might**: Khả năng nhỏ hơn may
- It might snow. (Có thể sẽ có tuyết - ít khả năng hơn may)

### Must / Have to

**Must**: Bắt buộc từ nội tại, chắc chắn
- You must study hard. (Bạn phải học chăm)
- She must be at home. (Chắc cô ấy đang ở nhà)

**Have to**: Bắt buộc từ bên ngoài
- I have to go to work. (Tôi phải đi làm - bắt buộc vì công việc)
- We have to follow the rules. (Chúng ta phải tuân thủ quy tắc)

### Should / Ought to

**Should**: Lời khuyên, nên
- You should eat more vegetables. (Bạn nên ăn nhiều rau)
- We should protect the environment. (Chúng ta nên bảo vệ môi trường)

**Ought to**: Như should nhưng formal hơn
- You ought to apologize. (Bạn nên xin lỗi)

### Would

1. Trong câu điều kiện (2nd Conditional)
- If I had money, I would buy a car.

2. Lời đề nghị lịch sự
- Would you like some coffee?

3. Thói quen trong quá khứ
- When I was young, I would play outside every day.

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Health advice
```
If you want to stay healthy, you should follow some important guidelines.
First, you must eat a balanced diet. You should include plenty of vegetables,
fruits, and whole grains in your meals. You ought to limit processed foods and
sugary drinks. Eating well can provide you with the energy you need and help
prevent many health problems.

Second, you must exercise regularly. You should try to exercise at least
30 minutes a day, five days a week. You could go for a walk, run, swim, or
join a gym. Regular exercise can help you maintain a healthy weight and reduce
the risk of heart disease.

You should also get enough sleep. Most adults need between 7 to 9 hours of
sleep each night. You might have trouble sleeping if you drink coffee late in
the day, so you should avoid caffeine in the evening. Getting enough rest is
essential for your physical and mental health.

You should avoid harmful habits. You ought to quit smoking if you smoke. You
should limit your alcohol consumption. These habits can seriously damage your
health over time.

Finally, you should take care of your mental health. You might feel stressed
sometimes, but you should find healthy ways to manage stress. You could try
meditation, yoga, or simply spending time with friends and family. You must
remember that mental health is just as important as physical health.

If you follow these suggestions, you will likely enjoy better health and
a higher quality of life.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Health Advice

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What should people do to stay healthy?
2. Why is exercise important?
3. What must we eat to stay healthy?
4. What habits should people avoid?
5. How can people manage stress?
6. What advice would you give to someone who wants to lose weight?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "There are several things people should do to stay healthy."

2. **Diet** (30 giây)
   - "First, you should/ought to..."
   - "You must..."

3. **Exercise** (30 giây)
   - "Second, you should..."
   - "You could..."

4. **Sleep & rest** (30 giây)
   - "You must get enough sleep..."

5. **Habits to avoid** (30 giây)
   - "You should avoid..."
   - "You ought to..."

6. **Mental health** (30 giây)
   - "You should also..."
   - "You might..."

### Sample Answer:
```
There are several things people should do to maintain good health. Let me
share some advice with you.

First, you must eat a balanced diet. You should include a variety of foods
in your meals, especially vegetables and fruits. You ought to limit processed
foods, sugar, and unhealthy fats. Eating well provides your body with the
nutrients it needs to function properly. You should also drink plenty of
water - at least 8 glasses a day.

Second, you should exercise regularly. You could aim for at least 30 minutes
of physical activity every day. You might go for a jog, swim, cycle, or join
a fitness class. Regular exercise helps you maintain a healthy weight,
strengthens your heart, and improves your mood. You should find activities
that you enjoy so you can stick with them.

You must also get enough sleep. Most adults need between 7 and 9 hours of
sleep each night. Good sleep is essential for your physical and mental health.
You should establish a regular sleep schedule and try to go to bed and wake
up at the same time every day.

You should avoid harmful habits. You ought to quit smoking if you smoke, as it
causes many serious health problems. You should also limit your alcohol
consumption. Smoking and excessive drinking can damage your liver, heart, and
increase your risk of cancer.

Finally, you should take care of your mental health. You might feel stressed
from time to time, but you should find healthy ways to manage it. You could
practice meditation, spend time in nature, or talk to friends and family.
You must remember that mental health is just as important as physical health.

By following these guidelines, you can enjoy better health and a higher quality
of life.
```

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **can**: khả năng hiện tại, cho phép
- **could**: khả năng quá khứ, đề nghị lịch sự
- **may/might**: khả năng, might ít khả năng hơn
- **must**: bắt buộc nội tại, chắc chắn
- **should/ought to**: lời khuyên, nên
- **would**: điều kiện, lịch sự, thói quen quá khứ
- Modal không chia theo ngôi, không chia tiếp diễn
