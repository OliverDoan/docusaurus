# Ngày 12 – Articles

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### A / An – Indefinite Articles

#### Khi nào dùng A/An?
1. Lần đầu nhắc đến danh từ (được định nghĩa chưa)
   - I saw a bird in the garden.
   - She has an old car.

2. Danh từ số ít, đếm được
   - a book, an apple, a student

3. Nghĩa "một", "một số", "một vài"
   - once a week
   - a few days

#### Quy tắc A vs An
- **a**: trước phụ âm (b, c, d, f, g, h, j, k, l, m, n, p, q, r, s, t, v, w, x, z)
  - a book, a cat, a university (u phát âm là /juː/)

- **an**: trước nguyên âm (a, e, i, o, u)
  - an apple, an egg, an hour (h câm), an honest man

### The – Definite Article

#### Khi nào dùng The?
1. Đã nhắc đến trước đó
   - I saw a bird. The bird was beautiful.

2. Chỉ định rõ, độc nhất
   - The sun rises in the east.
   - The capital of Vietnam is Hanoi.

3. Cụm từ chỉ vị trí, địa điểm
   - on the left, at the top, the morning
   - the center, the end

4. Đạo cụ, nhạc cụ
   - the piano, the guitar
   - the radio

5. Các danh từ đặc biệt
   - The internet, the news, the police

### Zero Article (Không dùng article)

#### Khi nào KHÔNG dùng article?
1. Danh từ số nhiều, đếm được
   - I like dogs. (chó nói chung)
   - Cars are expensive.

2. Danh từ không đếm được
   - I like music.
   - Water is essential.

3. Tên riêng (người, địa điểm, quốc gia số ít)
   - John, Mary, Vietnam, Tokyo, Monday
   - Ngoại lệ: The USA, The UK, The Netherlands

4. Bữa ăn, môn học
   - breakfast, lunch, dinner
   - mathematics, physics, history

5. Thể thao, ngôn ngữ
   - football, tennis, swimming
   - English, Vietnamese, Chinese

6. Công việc, tôn giáo
   - doctor, teacher, lawyer
   - Buddhism, Christianity

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Describe hometown
```
I would like to tell you about my hometown. I come from Hanoi, the capital
of Vietnam. Hanoi is a large city located in the northern part of the country.
It has a population of over 8 million people.

Hanoi is famous for its rich history and beautiful architecture. The Old Quarter
is one of the most popular tourist attractions. In the Old Quarter, you can
find many traditional houses, old temples, and narrow streets. It's a charming
area that preserves the traditional atmosphere of the city.

The city has many beautiful lakes. Hoan Kiem Lake is the most famous one.
It's located in the center of the city and is a popular place for walking
and relaxing. In the middle of the lake, there's a small island with the
Jade Temple.

Hanoi is also known for its delicious cuisine. Pho is the most famous dish.
It's a noodle soup with beef or chicken and aromatic herbs. Another popular
dish is bun cha, which is grilled pork with noodles. The food in Hanoi is
incredible and very affordable.

Transportation in Hanoi can be challenging. The traffic is often very heavy,
especially during rush hours. However, the city has been improving its
public transportation system. There are buses, taxis, and motorbike taxis.
A metro system is also under construction, which will make transportation
more convenient in the future.

The weather in Hanoi is interesting. The city has four distinct seasons.
Spring is pleasant with mild temperatures and flowers blooming everywhere.
Summer can be very hot and humid. Autumn is beautiful with clear skies and
comfortable temperatures. Winter can be cold, and sometimes it even snows.

I love my hometown. It's a city that combines tradition and modernity.
The people are friendly, the food is delicious, and there's always something
interesting to do. I'm proud to be from Hanoi.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Describe Your Hometown

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. Where is your hometown?
2. What is it famous for?
3. What are the main attractions?
4. What is the weather like there?
5. What kind of food is popular?
6. Do you like your hometown? Why?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (20 giây)
   - "I come from [city], a [size] city in [country]."

2. **Location & size** (20 giây)
   - "It's located in..."
   - "It has a population of..."

3. **Features & attractions** (45 giây)
   - "It's famous for..."
   - "The [place] is..."

4. **Food & culture** (30 giây)
   - "The food is..."
   - "People are..."

5. **Weather & conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "The weather is..."
   - "I love my hometown because..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I come from Hanoi, the capital city of Vietnam. It's located in the northern
part of the country, about 100 kilometers from the coast. Hanoi is a large
city with a population of over 8 million people.

Hanoi is famous for its rich history and cultural heritage. It was founded
over a thousand years ago and has served as the capital for most of Vietnam's
history. The Old Quarter is the most famous attraction. It's a historic area
with narrow streets, traditional houses, ancient temples, and busy markets.
Walking through the Old Quarter feels like stepping back in time.

The city also has many beautiful lakes and parks. Hoan Kiem Lake is the
heart of the city and a popular gathering place. On the lake, there's a
small island with the Turtle Tower, a historic symbol of Hanoi. West Lake
is another famous area with many restaurants and cafes along its shore.

Hanoi is known for its delicious cuisine. Pho, a noodle soup with beef or
chicken, is the most famous dish. Bun cha, grilled pork with noodles, is
another popular specialty. The street food culture is vibrant, and you can
find delicious snacks everywhere. The food is not only tasty but also very
affordable.

The weather in Hanoi is varied. The city experiences four distinct seasons.
Spring is pleasant with mild temperatures and blooming flowers. Summer is hot
and humid with frequent rain. Autumn is beautiful with clear skies and cool
breezes - many people consider it the best season. Winter can be cold,
sometimes with temperatures dropping to near freezing.

I love my hometown for many reasons. The blend of traditional and modern
architecture creates a unique atmosphere. The rich history and cultural heritage
give the city character. The food is amazing, and the people are friendly and
hospitable. I'm proud to call Hanoi my home.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

### Bài tập 1: Chọn article thích hợp
1. I saw ___ (a/an/the/-) cat yesterday. ___ cat was black.
2. He is ___ (a/an/the/-) doctor.
3. I love ___ (a/an/the/-) music.
4. ___ (A/An/The/-) sun rises in the east.
5. She lives in ___ (a/an/the/-) United States.

### Bài tập 2: Điền a/an/the hoặc nothing (-)
1. I want to be ___ teacher.
2. Can you pass me ___ salt?
3. ___ Nile is the longest river in Africa.
4. I studied at ___ university in Hanoi.
5. ___ dogs are loyal animals.
6. She plays ___ piano beautifully.
7. Let's have ___ dinner together.
8. ___ English is a global language.

### Bài tập 3: Sửa lỗi article
1. I saw the elephant in the zoo.
2. She has a old bicycle.
3. The water is essential for life.
4. He is a honest man.
5. I want to be the engineer.

### Bài tập 4: Viết câu mô tả quê hương
Viết 5 câu mô tả quê hương của bạn, sử dụng articles đúng cách.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **a/an**: lần đầu nhắc, số ít đếm được
- **the**: đã nhắc, độc nhất, định rõ
- **zero article**: số nhiều, không đếm được, tên riêng
- **a** trước phụ âm, **an** trước nguyên âm (chú ý cách phát âm)
- Không dùng article với: tên riêng, bữa ăn, môn học, thể thao
