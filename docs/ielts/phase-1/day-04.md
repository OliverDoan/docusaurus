# Ngày 4 – Past Continuous

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + was/were + V-ing
- **Phủ định**: S + was/were + not + V-ing
- **Nghi vấn**: Was/Were + S + V-ing?

### Quy tắc chia was/were
| Chủ ngữ | To be | Ví dụ |
|---------|-------|-------|
| I | was | I was studying. |
| He/She/It | was | He was working. |
| You/We/They | were | We were playing. |
| Tên riêng số ít | was | John was sleeping. |

### Khi nào dùng Past Continuous?
1. **Mô tả hành động đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm quá khứ**
   - At 8 PM last night, I was watching TV.
   - When I called him, he was having dinner.

2. **Hành động đang diễn ra khi một hành động khác xảy ra**
   - I was sleeping when the phone rang.
   - She was cooking when I arrived home.

3. **Mô tả hai hành động song song trong quá khứ**
   - While I was reading, my brother was playing video games.
   - They were studying while their friends were partying.

4. **Thiết lập bối cảnh, khung cảnh cho một câu chuyện**
   - The sun was shining. Birds were singing. I walked to the park.

### Kết hợp Past Continuous và Past Simple
```
[Chủ ngữ] + was/were + V-ing  +  when  +  [Chủ ngữ] + V2
(Hành động dài)            (khi)      (Hành động ngắn)

While  +  [Chủ ngữ] + was/were + V-ing  +  ,  +  [Chủ ngữ] + V2
 (trong khi)     (Hành động dài)                (Hành động ngắn)
```

Ví dụ:
- I **was sleeping** when the alarm **rang**. (Đang ngủ → chuông reo)
- While I **was cooking**, the power **went** out. (Đang nấu → mất điện)

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### A short story combining Past Simple and Past Continuous
```
Last weekend, something unusual happened to me. I was walking home from work
when I heard a strange noise. It was a rainy evening, and the streets were quiet.
I was listening to music on my headphones, so I didn't notice anything at first.

Suddenly, I saw a small dog running towards me. The dog was wet and shaking.
It was looking scared. I stopped walking and looked around. I was wondering
where the dog came from when a woman came running from the other direction.

"Thank goodness you found him!" she shouted. She was crying and looked very worried.
"I was searching for my dog everywhere," she said. The dog was her pet, and it had
escaped from her house. She was looking for him for more than an hour.

I helped her catch the dog, and we walked to her house together. She was thanking
me the whole way. When we arrived, she invited me for tea. We talked for a while,
and I learned that she lived alone and the dog was her only companion.

I went home feeling happy. It was a small experience, but it made me realize that
sometimes small acts of kindness can make a big difference.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Tell a Short Story

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. Can you tell me about a time something unexpected happened?
2. Have you ever had a surprising experience?
3. Tell me about a memorable event in your life.
4. What was the last interesting thing that happened to you?
5. Describe a time when you helped someone.

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Setting the scene** (30 giây) - Dùng Past Continuous
   - "It was..."
   - "I was... when..."

2. **The main event** (1 phút) - Kết hợp Past Simple & Past Continuous
   - "Suddenly, ..."
   - "While I was..., ..."

3. **The climax** (30 giây) - Kết hợp cả hai thì
   - Mô tả cao trào của câu chuyện

4. **The resolution** (30 giây) - Past Simple
   - "Finally, ..."

5. **Conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "In the end, ..." / "After that, ..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I would like to tell you about a surprising experience I had last summer.
It happened in July when I was traveling to Da Nang with my friends.

We were driving on the highway when our car suddenly stopped. It was a very
hot day, and we were about 50 kilometers from our destination. The driver tried
to restart the car, but it wouldn't work. We were all worried because we didn't
know what to do.

While we were standing by the road, a truck stopped behind us. The driver,
a middle-aged man, got out and asked if we needed help. We explained our
situation, and he offered to help. He was a mechanic, so he knew about cars.

He was checking our car for about 20 minutes while we were waiting nervously.
Finally, he found the problem and fixed it. He refused to accept any money
and just told us to be careful.

We arrived in Da Nang safely that evening. We were all very grateful to that
kind stranger. This experience taught me that there are still many good people
in the world who are willing to help others without expecting anything in return.
```

1. I ______ (study) when the phone rang.
2. She ______ (cook) dinner at 6 PM yesterday.
3. They ______ (not/watch) TV when I arrived.
4. What ______ you ______ (do) at this time yesterday?
5. He ______ (sleep) when the fire alarm went off.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- Cấu trúc: was/were + V-ing
- Dùng was với: I, he, she, it, tên số ít
- Dùng were với: you, we, they, danh từ số nhiều
- Kết hợp với Past Simple: was/were V-ing when... / While was/were V-ing, ...
- Dùng để thiết lập bối cảnh câu chuyện
