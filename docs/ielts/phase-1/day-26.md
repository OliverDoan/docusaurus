# Ngày 26 – Describe a Picture

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Cấu trúc để miêu tả tranh ảnh

#### Present Continuous (đang diễn ra)
```
In the picture, people are doing various activities.
A man is reading a book on the bench.
Children are playing in the park.
```

#### Present Simple (tính chất chung)
```
The picture shows a beautiful park.
There are many people enjoying their time.
The scene looks peaceful and relaxing.
```

#### Prepositions (vị trí)
```
on the left / on the right
in the foreground / in the background
at the bottom / at the top
in the corner / in the center
next to / beside / behind / in front of
```

### Các mẫu câu miêu tả

#### Giới thiệu chung
```
This picture depicts a scene of...
The photograph shows...
In this image, we can see...
```

#### Miêu tả chi tiết
```
On the left, there is...
In the foreground, I can see...
In the background, there are...
At the center of the picture...
```

#### Miêu tả hành động
```
The people in the picture are...
A man is doing...
The children seem to be...
They appear to be enjoying...
```

#### Đoán giả
```
It seems that...
I guess that...
It looks like...
They might be...
```

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Describing a park scene
```
This picture depicts a lively scene on a sunny afternoon in a park.
In the foreground, I can see a large green grass area where
several people are spending their leisure time.

On the left side of the picture, there is an elderly man who
is sitting on a wooden bench. He appears to be reading a newspaper
and seems to enjoy the peaceful atmosphere. Beside him, a young
woman is jogging along a winding path that goes through the
park. She looks energetic and focused on her exercise.

In the center of the photograph, a group of children are playing
a game of tag. They are running and laughing, clearly having a
great time. A few parents are watching over them from nearby
benches, looking relaxed and content as they supervise their
children's play.

On the right side, a family is having a picnic on a checkered
tablecloth. A mother is setting out food while a father is
pouring drinks into cups. Two children are eagerly waiting for
their meal. The scene looks warm and happy, showing family
bonding time.

In the background, tall trees are providing shade, and the sky
is bright blue with a few white clouds. Sunlight is filtering
through the leaves, creating a beautiful pattern of light and
shadow on the ground. There's also a small lake where some ducks
are swimming, adding life to the scene.

The overall atmosphere of this picture is one of relaxation and
joy. Everyone in the scene seems to be enjoying their time
in this pleasant park setting. The photograph captures a perfect
day out in nature where people can escape from their busy
lives and appreciate the simple pleasures of outdoor activities.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Describe a Picture

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What can you see in the picture?
2. What are the people doing?
3. Where is this picture taken?
4. What is the atmosphere like?
5. What do you think happened before/after this picture?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "This picture depicts a scene of..."
   - "In this photograph, I can see..."

2. **Foreground/Details** (45 giây)
   - "In the foreground..."
   - "On the left/right..."
   - Dùng Present Continuous

3. **Background** (30 giây)
   - "In the background..."
   - Dùng Present Continuous

4. **Atmosphere & feelings** (30 giây)
   - "The atmosphere seems..."
   - "The people appear to be..."

5. **Assumptions/Conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "It looks like..."
   - "Overall, this picture shows..."

### Sample Answer:
```
This picture depicts a busy coffee shop on a typical weekday
morning. In the foreground, I can see several people who are
enjoying their time in this cozy establishment.

On the left side of the photograph, a young woman is sitting at
a small round table by the window. She appears to be working on
her laptop, possibly a student on professional who is utilizing
the coffee shop as a workspace. A steaming cup of coffee sits
beside her computer, and she looks focused on her task.

In the center of the picture, the bar counter is visible where
a barista is preparing drinks. He is skillfully operating the
espresso machine and seems to be in the middle of making a
complicated order for a customer. Two people are waiting patiently
at the counter, looking at the menu boards above the barista.

On the right side, two men are having an animated conversation
at another table. They are leaning forward and gesturing, clearly
engaged in a serious or passionate discussion. Coffee cups
are in front of both men, indicating they have been there for
some time. One of them is occasionally checking his watch.

In the background, I can see other customers at various tables.
Some are reading books or newspapers, while others are chatting
with their companions. Large windows along one wall let in plenty
of natural light, creating a warm and inviting atmosphere.
Artistic paintings on the walls add character to the space.

The overall mood of this picture is relaxed yet productive. The
people seem to appreciate the comfortable environment that allows
them to work, socialize, or simply enjoy a good cup of coffee.
It looks like a place where community members gather regularly,
making it more than just a business - it's a social hub.

I imagine this picture was taken on a weekday morning because
many people appear to be working or studying rather than
socializing recreationally. The coffee shop seems to serve as a
second office or study space for these individuals who need a
change of scenery from their usual workplaces.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

### Bài tập 1: Điền Present Continuous hoặc Present Simple
1. In the picture, people ______ (enjoy) their time.
2. A man ______ (sit) on the bench.
3. Children ______ (play) in the park.
4. The scene ______ (look) peaceful.
5. Someone ______ (read) a book.

### Bài tập 2: Điền giới từ vị trí
1. ________ the left, there is a tree. (On/In/At)
2. In ________, I can see people. (foreground/background/center)
3. ________ the background, the sky is blue. (In/On/At)
4. ________ the right, children are playing. (On/In/At)
5. ________ the center, a man is sitting. (In/On/At)

### Bài tập 3: Viết câu miêu tả tranh ảnh
1. ở phía trái, có một người đàn ông đang ngồi
   → ________________________________________________

2. trong tiền cảnh, trẻ em đang chơi
   → ________________________________________________

3. ở hậu cảnh, có những cái cây
   → ________________________________________________

4. có vẻ như họ đang tận hưởng
   → ________________________________________________

5. bức tranh trông rất bình yên
   → ________________________________________________

### Bài tập 4: Mô tả một cảnh
Viết 5-7 câu miêu tả một cảnh (công viên, nhà hàng, côn),
dùng Present Continuous và giới từ vị trí.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Present Continuous**: hành động đang diễn ra trong tranh ảnh
- **Present Simple**: tính chất chung, mô tả
- **Giới từ vị trí**: on the left/right, in foreground/background, at center
- **Giới thiệu**: This picture depicts/shows...
- **Đoán giả**: It seems that..., It looks like...
- **Cấu trúc**: In the foreground/background, On the left/right...
