# Ngày 21 – Mock Speaking Test (IELTS Speaking Part 1 & Part 2)

## 📚 IELTS Speaking Format Overview

### IELTS Speaking Structure (11-14 phút)

| Part | Thời gian | Nội dung |
|------|----------|----------|
| Part 1 | 4-5 phút | Câu hỏi về quê hương, sở thích, công việc... |
| Part 2 | 3-4 phút chuẩn bị | Nói về chủ đề cụ thể (1 phút chuẩn bị + 1-2 phút nói) |
| Part 3 | 4-5 phút | Thảo luận sâu hơn về chủ đề Part 2 |

## IELTS Speaking Part 1

### Thời gian phân bổ
- 1 phút: Câu hỏi chung
- 1 phút: Chủ đề quen thuộc
- 1 phút: Chủ đề quan hệ
- 1-2 phút: Chủ đề tương lai, kế hoạch

### Các chủ đề phổ biến trong Part 1
1. **Work/Studies**: What do you do/study?
2. **Hometown**: Where are you from?
3. **Accommodation**: Do you live in a house or flat?
4. **Free time**: What do you do in your free time?
5. **Food**: What is your favorite food?
6. **Transport**: How do you usually travel?
7. **Weather**: What kind of weather do you like?
8. **Hobbies**: What are your hobbies?
9. **Family**: Do you have a large family?
10. **Friends**: Do you prefer spending time alone or with friends?

### Strategy cho Part 1
- Câu trả lời ngắn (2-3 câu)
- Trả lời trực tiếp, không lan man
- Mở rộng bằng lý do (because, since, as)
- Dùng từ nối: Actually, To be honest, Well
- Cố gắng trả lời cho cả 2 vế (positive và negative)

## IELTS Speaking Part 2

### Thời gian phân bổ
- 1 phút: Chuẩn bị (đọc chủ đề + ghi ý)
- 2 phút: Nói (không dừng, không nhìn ghi chép liên tục)
- 1 phút: Câu hỏi theo dõi (tùy examiner)

### Chủ đề Part 2 phổ biến
1. **Describe a person**: A person you admire, A family member...
2. **Describe a place**: A city you visited, A historical building...
3. **Describe an object**: A gift you received, A thing you bought...
4. **Describe an event**: A memorable day, A festival...
5. **Describe an activity**: A hobby, A sport you enjoy...
6. **Describe a media**: A book you read, A movie you watched...

### Structure cho Part 2 (2 phút)
1. **Introduction** (15-20 giây)
   - "I would like to talk about..."
   - Giới thiệu chủ đề trực tiếp

2. **Description** (45-60 giây)
   - Mô tả chi tiết, đặc điểm nổi bật
   - Dùng từ miêu tả: beautiful, spacious, charming...

3. **Story/Experience** (45-60 giây)
   - Câu chuyện liên quan, kinh nghiệm cá nhân
   - Dùng thì phù hợp

4. **Feelings & Opinions** (30-45 giây)
   - Cảm xúc, đánh giá cá nhân
   - Dùng từ miêu tả cảm xúc: exciting, memorable, challenging...

5. **Conclusion** (15-20 giây)
   - "That's why..." / "Overall..."
   - Tóm tắt vì sao chủ đề quan trọng

## 📖 Từ vựng IELTS Speaking

| Từ loại | Từ vựng |
|---------|----------|
| **Mô tả** | attractive, beautiful, charming, modern, traditional, spacious, crowded, quiet |
| **Cảm xúc** | exciting, memorable, enjoyable, challenging, stressful, fascinating |
| **Kinh nghiệm** | experience, opportunity, challenge, achievement, milestone |
| **Đánh giá** | important, significant, valuable, beneficial, influential, essential |
| **Bổ sung** | also, besides, in addition, furthermore, moreover |
| **So sánh** | better, worse, more, less, rather than, prefer, favorite |
| **Kết luận** | overall, in conclusion, to sum up, all in all |

## 💬 Mock Speaking Test – Part 1

### Sample Questions & Answers

**Q1: What is your hometown like?**
```
My hometown is Hanoi, which is the capital of Vietnam. It's a large
city with a rich history and beautiful architecture. The Old Quarter is
particularly charming with its narrow streets and traditional houses.
```

**Q2: Do you work or are you a student?**
```
I'm a software developer. I work for a technology company in Hanoi.
I've been in this position for about two years now. I find the work
both challenging and rewarding.
```

**Q3: What do you do in your free time?**
```
In my free time, I enjoy reading books and watching movies. I also
like playing sports, especially badminton and swimming. On weekends,
I usually spend time with my family or friends.
```

**Q4: Do you prefer living in a city or in the countryside?**
```
I prefer living in a city because it offers more convenience and
opportunities. However, I also enjoy visiting the countryside
occasionally to relax and enjoy nature.
```

**Q5: What kind of music do you like?**
```
I enjoy various types of music, but I particularly like classical
music and jazz. I find them very relaxing and they help me
concentrate when I'm working.
```

## 💬 Mock Speaking Test – Part 2

### Sample Topic: Describe a gift you received that was important to you

**You should say:**
- What the gift was
- Who gave it to you
- When you received it
- Explain why it was important

```
I would like to talk about a laptop computer that my parents gave me
when I started university. This gift was extremely important to me for
several reasons.

The laptop was a mid-range model that my parents carefully chose.
They knew I would need a computer for my studies, especially for
programming assignments and research. It wasn't the most expensive
model on the market, but it was perfect for my needs as a student.

I received this gift just before my first semester at university.
My parents gave it to me as a graduation present after I finished high
school. They presented it to me during a small family celebration,
which made the moment even more special.

This laptop was important to me for many reasons. First, it became
an essential tool for my education. I used it every day for taking
notes in class, writing assignments, and conducting research. Without
it, my university studies would have been much more difficult.

Second, this gift represented my parents' love and support.
They had saved money for a long time to afford this laptop. Their
sacrifice showed how much they believed in me and wanted me to succeed.
This gave me confidence and motivation to do my best.

Furthermore, this laptop allowed me to learn new skills beyond my
studies. I started programming and creating small projects in my
free time. These skills later helped me get my current job as a
software developer.

I still have this laptop, although I've upgraded to a newer model
since then. However, I keep it as a reminder of my parents'
generosity and the beginning of my educational journey. It holds a
special place in my heart.

In conclusion, this laptop was more than just a technological device.
It was a symbol of my family's support, a tool for my education,
and the starting point of my career. That's why this gift is so
meaningful to me.
```

## 📝 Practice Tips

### Tips cho Part 1
1. Câu trả lời 2-3 câu, không dài
2. Mở rộng bằng because, since, as
3. Cố gắng trả lời cả 2 vế
4. Dùng từ nối: Actually, Well, To be honest
5. Chỉnh nhịp độ, không quá nhanh/quá chậm

### Tips cho Part 2
1. Chuẩn bị hiệu quả (1 phút)
2. Nói không dừng (2 phút liên tục)
3. Dùng cấu trúc có tổ chức
4. Đa dạng từ, dùng từ hiếu
5. Không nhìn vào ghi chép liên tục
6. Cố gắng nói đến hết thời gian

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **Part 1**: Câu hỏi quê hương, sở thích (4-5 phút)
- **Part 2**: Mô tả chủ đề cụ thể (3-4 phút chuẩn bị)
- **Strategy Part 1**: Câu ngắn, trả lời trực tiếp
- **Strategy Part 2**: Chuẩn bị kỹ, nói liên tục, có tổ chức
- **Từ vựng**: Cố gắng dùng từ hiếu và đa dạng
