# Ngày 7 – Future (Will / Going to)

## 📚 Ngữ pháp chi tiết

### Will

#### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + will + V (nguyên thể)
- **Phủ định**: S + will not + V (won't)
- **Nghi vấn**: Will + S + V?

#### Khi nào dùng Will?
1. **Dự đoán không có bằng chứng**
   - I think it will rain tomorrow.
   - She will probably get the job.

2. **Quyết định tức thời, ngay lúc nói**
   - I'll help you with that.
   - Don't worry, I'll do it.

3. **Hứa hẹn, cam kết**
   - I will always love you.
   - I'll never forget this moment.

4. **Lời mời, đề nghị**
   - Will you marry me?
   - Will you have dinner with me?

5. **Đầu tiên (First Conditional)**
   - If it rains, I will stay home.

### Be going to

#### Cấu trúc
- **Khẳng định**: S + am/is/are + going to + V
- **Phủ định**: S + am/is/are + not + going to + V
- **Nghi vấn**: Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V?

#### Khi nào dùng Be going to?
1. **Kế hoạch đã có, ý định từ trước**
   - I am going to visit my parents next week.
   - She is going to study abroad next year.

2. **Dự đoán có bằng chứng hiển nhiên**
   - Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
   - The bus is coming. It's going to stop here.

3. **Sắp xảy ra rất gần**
   - The movie is going to start soon.
   - She is going to have a baby.

### Phân biệt Will vs Going to

| Will | Be going to |
|------|-------------|
| Quyết định tức thời | Kế hoạch đã có từ trước |
| Dự đoán không bằng chứng | Dự đoán có bằng chứng |
| Promise, offer | Sắp xảy ra gần |
| "I'll help you" | "I'm going to buy a car" |

## 💬 Ví dụ mẫu

### Future plans
```
I have many plans for the future. First, I am going to finish my current
project at work next month. After that, I'm going to take a short vacation.
I've already booked tickets to Da Nang. I'm going to stay there for a week.

In the next few years, I intend to advance my career. I plan to learn more
about artificial intelligence and data science. I think these fields will be
very important in the future. I will probably take some online courses to
improve my skills.

My long-term goal is to start my own business. I would like to create a tech
startup that helps people in my country. I believe that with hard work and
determination, I will achieve this dream. I'm going to start saving money
for this purpose.

I also want to travel more. I plan to visit several European countries in the
next five years. I'll definitely visit France, Italy, and Spain. I'm looking
forward to exploring different cultures and meeting new people.
```

## 🗣 Speaking Practice: Future Plans (5 years ahead)

### Câu hỏi gợi ý:
1. What are you going to do next year?
2. Do you have any plans for the next 5 years?
3. What are your career goals?
4. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
5. What will you do if you achieve your goals?
6. What are your dreams for the future?

### Speaking Structure:
1. **Introduction** (15 giây)
   - "I have several plans for the next 5 years."

2. **Short-term plans (1 year)** (40 giây)
   - "I'm going to..." / "I will..."

3. **Medium-term plans (2-3 years)** (40 giây)
   - "In the next few years, I plan to..."

4. **Long-term goals (5 years)** (40 giây)
   - "My long-term goal is to..."
   - "In 5 years, I hope to..."

5. **Conclusion** (30 giây)
   - "I believe these plans will help me..."

### Sample Answer:
```
I have several plans for the next 5 years. In the short term, I am going to
focus on improving my English skills. I'm going to take the IELTS exam next
year because I need it for my career advancement. After that, I'm going to
apply for a better position at my current company.

In the next 2 to 3 years, I plan to develop my professional skills further.
I intend to learn new technologies, especially in the field of artificial
intelligence. I think AI will be very important in the future, and I want to
be prepared. I will probably take some online courses and get certifications
in these areas.

My long-term goal for the next 5 years is to become a team leader or manager.
I hope to have gained enough experience and skills to lead a team of developers.
I would like to mentor younger colleagues and contribute to important projects.
If I achieve this goal, I will be very proud and motivated to continue growing.

Besides my career, I also have personal goals. I plan to travel to at least
three different countries in the next 5 years. I would like to experience
different cultures and improve my language skills. I'm definitely going to save
money regularly to make these trips possible.

Overall, I believe that with hard work and determination, I will achieve these
plans. I'm excited about the future and ready to work hard to reach my goals.
```

## 📝 Bài tập thực hành

### Bài tập 1: Chọn will hoặc be going to
1. I (will / am going to) visit my grandmother next week.
2. Look at the sky! It (will / is going to) rain.
3. I've decided. I (will / am going to) learn English.
4. Wait a moment. I (will / am) going to help you.
5. She (will / is going to) probably get the job.

### Bài tập 2: Chia động từ đúng dạng
1. I ______ (visit) my parents next week.
2. She ______ (probably) pass the exam.
3. They ______ (not/go) to the party.
4. ______ you ______ (help) me tomorrow?
5. He ______ (definitely) come.

## 🔑 Tóm tắt key points
- **will**: quyết định tức thời, dự đoán không bằng chứng, hứa hẹn
- **be going to**: kế hoạch có từ trước, dự đoán có bằng chứng
- **will**: "I'll help you", "It will probably rain"
- **be going to**: "I'm going to buy a car", "Look! It's going to rain"
- Cả hai đều dùng cho tương lai, nhưng ngữ cảnh khác nhau
